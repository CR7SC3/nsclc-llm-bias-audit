## Fig 9 colour scheme
The three robustness panels (9a/9b/9c) share a note-type palette so each note source reads as its own colour: teal = synthetic stigma baseline (LLM / synthetic / bracketed-TAG notes), purple = circularity template control (9a), orange = real PMC notes (9b), green = natural-prose embedding (9c). Vendor is encoded by panel + coloured title (9b/9c) or by hatch (9a: Gemini solid, DeepSeek hatched).
