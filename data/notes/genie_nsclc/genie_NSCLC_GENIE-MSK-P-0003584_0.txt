**HPI:**
This is a 59-year-old individual who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient reports the onset of a persistent, dry cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion over the last several months. This was initially attributed to the patient's long-standing history of current tobacco use. However, due to worsening symptoms and the development of mild fatigue, the patient sought medical evaluation approximately six weeks ago. The patient is accompanied to the visit today and appears in no acute distress. Functionally, the patient is fully ambulatory and managing all activities of daily living with some limitations due to shortness of breath, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest radiograph was abnormal, which prompted a contrast-enhanced CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis on [De-identified date]. Imaging revealed a large, spiculated mass in the upper lobe of the right lung. Multiple smaller nodules were also identified in the contralateral (left) lung, concerning for metastatic disease. Mediastinal lymphadenopathy was also noted. No suspicious lesions were seen in the liver, adrenal glands, or osseous structures.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right lung mass. Pathologic examination of the specimen confirmed a diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was subsequently performed to complete the initial workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
-   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alterations were identified. The tumor was found to be negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **PD-L1 Expression:** Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was strongly positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 59-year-old with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup confirmed a primary right lung mass with contralateral pulmonary metastases, consistent with AJCC Stage IV disease. The patient is a current smoker with an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%) and the absence of common actionable driver mutations. The case was discussed in detail with the patient.
