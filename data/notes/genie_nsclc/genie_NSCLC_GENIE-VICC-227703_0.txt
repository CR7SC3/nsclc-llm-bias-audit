**HPI:**
This is a 70-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit <1 year ago) who presents for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to an outside provider in [Month], [Year] with a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea that had progressed over several weeks. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient denies hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or significant weight loss. The patient remains fully ambulatory, independent in all activities of daily living, and maintains an excellent ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed. This confirmed the presence of a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated bulky mediastinal lymphadenopathy, including right paratracheal and subcarinal nodes measuring up to 2.5 cm in short-axis.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was subsequently performed on [Date], [Year]. Pathology returned as invasive adenocarcinoma.

Staging workup was completed with a PET/CT scan, which demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in the right paratracheal and subcarinal lymph nodes, consistent with the findings on the diagnostic CT. No FDG-avid lesions suggestive of distant metastatic disease in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or skeleton were identified. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the core biopsy. Results are as follows:
-   An actionable driver mutation was detected in **KRAS (G12C)**.
-   No pathogenic mutations were identified in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, or ERBB2.
-   Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was determined to be **intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb)**.
-   PD-L1 expression was not tested on this specimen.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 70-year-old former smoker who presents for initial evaluation of a newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the presence of a KRAS G12C mutation. The patient has an excellent performance status and is here to discuss the diagnosis and potential management options.
