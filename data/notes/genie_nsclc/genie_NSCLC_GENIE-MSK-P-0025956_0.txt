**History of Present Illness:**

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A comprehensive staging evaluation was completed prior to this consultation.

A CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with intravenous contrast was performed. This identified a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. There was significant associated ipsilateral mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy, with the largest node measuring 3.0 cm in short axis. No suspicious hepatic, adrenal, or other distant visceral lesions were identified.

A subsequent PET/CT scan confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right hilar and mediastinal nodal stations (4R, 7). No other sites of FDG-avid disease suggestive of distant metastases were identified.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for central nervous system staging and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed. Final pathology confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.
