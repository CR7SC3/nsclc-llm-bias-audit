**HPI:**
This is an initial oncology consultation for a 51-year-old with a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a current smoker. The patient presented to their primary care provider approximately six weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea. Initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient has had no significant weight loss, hemoptysis, or chest pain. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0, with the patient continuing to work and perform all activities of daily living without restriction. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed following the abnormal chest X-ray. This demonstrated a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed prominent right hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy, with the largest node measuring 2.5 cm in the paratracheal station. No distant visceral or bone metastases were identified.

A PET/CT scan was subsequently performed for staging. This confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right hilar and mediastinal lymph nodes, consistent with the CT findings. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastatic spread.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for neurologic staging and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastasis.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS). Biopsies were obtained from the primary right upper lobe mass and the right paratracheal lymph node (station 4R). Pathology from both sites confirmed adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy. The nodal involvement confirms locally advanced disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tissue biopsy specimen.
- **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** PD-L1 tumor proportion score (TPS) is <1%.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB is high at >16 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 51-year-old, treatment-naive patient with a new diagnosis of Stage IIIA (cT2bN2M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup, including PET/CT and brain MRI, shows no evidence of distant metastatic disease. Molecular profiling is negative for targetable driver mutations, with PD-L1 expression <1% and a high tumor mutational burden. The patient presents for initial consultation to review these findings.
