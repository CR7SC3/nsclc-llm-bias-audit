**HPI:**
This is an initial medical oncology consultation for a 63-year-old with a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker, having quit within the last year. The patient presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and intermittent right-sided chest discomfort. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient has a good performance status, remaining active and independent with all activities of daily living.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which identified a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also revealed prominent right hilar and subcarinal lymphadenopathy, with the largest node measuring 2.2 cm in short axis. No evidence of distant visceral or bone metastases was identified on the CT portion of the study.

A subsequent PET/CT was performed for staging. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right hilar and subcarinal lymph nodes, consistent with the CT findings. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastases. A dedicated MRI of the brain was also obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS). An EBUS-guided fine-needle aspiration was performed on the right upper lobe mass and a subcarinal lymph node. Pathology from both sites returned as adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical stage IIIA (T2aN2M0) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tissue obtained from the biopsy.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- A loss-of-function mutation was identified in STK11.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 63-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic, stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling is notable for an STK11 loss-of-function mutation and the absence of other targetable driver alterations.
