**HPI:**
This is a 60-year-old individual with a recent history of smoking (quit <1 year ago) who presents for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately one month prior to presentation, when they began experiencing persistent headaches and mild word-finding difficulties. These neurological symptoms prompted an evaluation in the emergency department, which initiated a diagnostic workup. The patient reports some associated fatigue but remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 1. The patient has no significant prior oncologic history and has not received any prior therapy for this diagnosis. They are here today to review the diagnostic findings and discuss the situation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
The patient's evaluation began with a brain MRI to investigate the new neurological symptoms. This study revealed multiple enhancing intracranial lesions, concerning for metastatic disease.

A subsequent staging PET/CT scan performed on [De-identified Date] identified a 4.2 cm hypermetabolic primary mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. The scan also confirmed hypermetabolic activity corresponding to the known brain lesions and further identified a 2.5 cm hypermetabolic lesion in the left adrenal gland, consistent with an additional site of distant metastatic disease.

To obtain a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic analysis of the specimen confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry were performed on the lung biopsy tissue with the following key results:
-   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in *EGFR*, *ALK*, *ROS1*, *BRAF*, *MET* exon 14, *RET*, *NTRK*, *KRAS*, and *ERBB2*.
-   **PD-L1 Expression:** PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was strongly positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was found to be high at >16 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 60-year-old former smoker who presents as treatment-naive with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The diagnostic workup confirms metastatic disease involving the brain and the left adrenal gland. Comprehensive molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the absence of any targetable driver mutations, but shows high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%) and a high Tumor Mutational Burden. The patient has a good performance status and is here for initial consultation.
