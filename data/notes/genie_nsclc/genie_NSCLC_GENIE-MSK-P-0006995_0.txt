**HPI:**
This is a 52-year-old who presents for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to an outside facility approximately one month ago with several weeks of progressive shortness of breath and right-sided pleuritic chest pain. An initial chest x-ray revealed a large right pleural effusion. The patient has remained symptomatic with dyspnea on exertion but is fully ambulatory and managing all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 1. A comprehensive diagnostic workup was initiated. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified date], which demonstrated a 3.8 cm primary mass in the right lower lobe of the lung. This was associated with extensive, loculated right-sided pleural thickening and a large pleural effusion. The scan also revealed multiple enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes and suspicious contralateral supraclavicular lymphadenopathy.

A subsequent PET/CT scan on [De-identified date] confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right lung mass, the right pleura, and in multiple nodal stations including the mediastinum and left supraclavicular fossa, consistent with distant metastatic disease.

A staging MRI of the brain was performed on [De-identified date] and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified date], the patient underwent a CT-guided biopsy of a right-sided pleural nodule. Pathologic analysis of the tissue specimen confirmed invasive, poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemical stains were positive for TTF-1 and Napsin-A, supporting a lung primary origin.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the pleural biopsy specimen.
- **Actionable driver mutation detected:** EGFR exon 19 deletion (sensitizing).
- **No pathogenic mutations were identified in:** ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 expression:** Not tested.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 52-year-old lifetime never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging studies confirmed a primary right lower lobe mass with metastatic involvement of the pleura and distant (contralateral supraclavicular) lymph nodes. Brain imaging is negative for metastatic disease. Molecular profiling is notable for a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion. The patient is here for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and establish a plan for management.
