This was initially self-managed but did not resolve, prompting a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest radiograph revealed a right upper lobe opacity, leading to further diagnostic evaluation. The patient reports no significant hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or unintentional weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0, and they continue to work and perform all activities of daily living without restriction. They are here today to discuss the findings and establish care.
