**HPI:**
This is an initial oncology consultation for a 73-year-old, a lifetime never-smoker, who presents for evaluation of newly diagnosed metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive fatigue. More recently, they noted the onset of new, nagging low back pain, which prompted an evaluation by their primary care provider. The patient maintains a good performance status, fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, though activities are somewhat limited by fatigue.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe mass, leading to a staging CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast. This study confirmed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, extensive mediastinal and hilar adenopathy, multiple sub-centimeter hypodense liver lesions consistent with metastases, and several sclerotic and lytic osseous lesions in the thoracic spine and pelvis, correlating with the patient's back pain. A dedicated MRI of the brain was performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the primary right upper lobe lung mass on [De-identified Date]. Pathologic evaluation of the specimen confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry were performed on the biopsy specimen.
-   **Histology:** Adenocarcinoma
-   **PD-L1:** Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) is ≥50%.
-   **Molecular Profiling:** An actionable driver mutation was identified: MET exon 14 skipping mutation.
-   **Additional Markers:** Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 73-year-old who is treatment-naive with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (cT2b N2 M1c) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology, with metastatic disease to the bone, liver, and distant lymph nodes. The patient is a never-smoker with a good performance status. Molecular profiling is notable for a MET exon 14 skipping mutation and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The patient is here today to discuss these findings.
