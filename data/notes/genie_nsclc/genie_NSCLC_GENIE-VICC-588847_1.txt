**History of Present Illness**

There were no overt respiratory symptoms such as cough or dyspnea noted at that time. Given the neurological symptoms, initial evaluation included brain imaging. **Diagnostic Workup**
Initial brain MRI demonstrated multiple enhancing lesions concerning for metastatic disease. Subsequent chest imaging, including a CT scan of the chest, revealed a primary mass in the right upper lobe, along with mediastinal lymphadenopathy. A PET-CT scan confirmed hypermetabolic activity in the primary lung lesion, mediastinal nodes, and the previously identified brain lesions, with no other sites of distant metastatic disease detected. Biopsy of the lung mass, performed via bronchoscopy, confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer with adenocarcinoma histology. Based on the presence of distant metastases to the brain, the patient is staged as AJCC Stage IV.
