**HPI:**
This is a 67-year-old individual with an excellent performance status (ECOG 0) who is referred for initial oncologic consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial course of antibiotics prescribed by their primary care provider did not lead to resolution of symptoms. The patient denies any associated fevers, chills, night sweats, hemoptysis, or unintentional weight loss. Given the persistence of the cough, a chest X-ray was obtained, which was notable for a right upper lobe lung nodule, prompting further investigation. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a diagnostic CT of the chest with contrast was performed on [De-identified date]. This study confirmed the presence of a 3.5 cm spiculated solid mass in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. There was no significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy identified.

On [De-identified date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue specimen confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy.

Subsequent staging evaluation included a full-body PET/CT scan performed on [De-identified date]. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity confined to the known right upper lobe primary tumor. Critically, there was no evidence of FDG-avid hilar, mediastinal, or supraclavicular lymphadenopathy. No distant metastatic disease was identified in the chest, abdomen, or pelvis. A dedicated MRI of the brain with contrast was also performed for central nervous system staging and was negative for intracranial metastases.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient's disease is classified as clinical stage T2a N0 M0, corresponding to AJCC 8th edition Stage IIA.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the core biopsy. No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was found to be negative for mutations or fusions involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was not tested, consistent with the standard of care at the time of the diagnostic evaluation. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 67-year-old, treatment-naive never-smoker who presents for evaluation of a newly diagnosed Stage IIA (cT2a N0 M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, of the right upper lobe. Molecular profiling did not identify any actionable oncogenic drivers. The patient has an excellent performance status and is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
