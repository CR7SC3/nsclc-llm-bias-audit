**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP**
A diagnostic workup was initiated following the abnormal chest X-ray.

A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated solid mass in the right upper lobe. No significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was identified.

For staging purposes, a PET/CT was performed. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity localized to the primary right upper lobe mass. There was no evidence of hypermetabolic activity in regional lymph nodes or in any distant visceral or osseous sites to suggest metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass was performed. Histopathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of primary lung adenocarcinoma.
