**HPI:**
This is a 58-year-old individual with a history of a persistent, non-productive cough over the past several months, who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation. The patient was initially evaluated by a primary care provider, which led to a chest radiograph revealing a right upper lobe lung nodule. This prompted a referral for further diagnostic workup. The patient has no significant smoking history (never smoker) and is otherwise active with an excellent performance status. Review of systems is negative for hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or unintentional weight loss. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A comprehensive staging evaluation was undertaken following the initial abnormal chest X-ray.

A CT of the chest with contrast confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm solid mass in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy. The contralateral lung was clear.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe mass. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the regional lymph nodes or at any distant sites, including the liver, adrenal glands, or skeleton.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed as part of the standard staging workup for non-small cell lung cancer and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass was performed by Interventional Radiology. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, with features most consistent with adenocarcinoma histology.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor biopsy specimen.
- An activating somatic mutation was identified in the Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) gene, specifically an L858R point mutation in exon 21.
- Testing was negative for alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be low (<2 mutations/megabase).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 58-year-old never-smoker who presents for initial oncologic evaluation of a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The diagnosis is specifically a Stage IIA (cT2b N0 M0) adenocarcinoma of the right lung, notable for the presence of a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation. The patient has completed staging which confirms localized disease, and is seen today to discuss the findings and potential management approaches.
