**HPI:**

The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent dry cough and new-onset, localized pain in the right posterior chest wall. The pain was initially attributed to a musculoskeletal strain but worsened over several weeks, prompting a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious opacity in the right lung, which led to further cross-sectional imaging. The patient reports some associated fatigue but has maintained their weight and appetite. They remain fully ambulatory and are managing all activities of daily living, though the chest wall pain is a limiting factor. ECOG performance status is estimated to be 1.
