**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old individual with a history of former tobacco use (quit >1 year ago) who is referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to an outside facility several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially managed symptomatically but did not resolve, prompting a chest X-ray which revealed a right-sided lung opacity. The patient reports feeling generally well otherwise, with a good energy level and no significant weight loss, though notes some mild dyspnea with strenuous activity. Performance status is excellent at ECOG 0-1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed. This confirmed the presence of a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also identified several enlarged right-sided mediastinal and subcarinal lymph nodes, concerning for regional involvement.

For comprehensive staging, a PET/CT was obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe primary tumor. Avid uptake was also noted in ipsilateral mediastinal (4R) and subcarinal (7) lymph nodes. Critically, the PET/CT showed no evidence of FDG-avid disease below the diaphragm or elsewhere to suggest distant metastatic spread. A staging MRI of the brain was subsequently performed and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathology confirmed a diagnosis of primary lung adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the biopsy specimen was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing. The results revealed the presence of a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion. Further testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, ERBB2, and KRAS. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 55-year-old individual with a new diagnosis of Stage IIIA non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma, characterized by a primary right upper lobe tumor with ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal nodal involvement, and no evidence of distant metastatic disease. Molecular analysis is notable for an actionable EGFR exon 19 deletion. The patient presents to clinic today for initial consultation to discuss these findings.
