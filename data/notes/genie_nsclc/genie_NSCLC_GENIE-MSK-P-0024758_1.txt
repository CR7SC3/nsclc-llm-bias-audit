**HPI:**
This 73-year-old with a significant former smoking history (quit over one year ago) is seen today for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. An initial chest radiograph demonstrated a right-sided pulmonary opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise active with an ECOG performance status of 1 and has had no significant weight loss, hemoptysis, or chest pain.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed, which revealed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also identified bulky ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy, concerning for regional metastatic disease.

For comprehensive staging, a PET/CT was subsequently obtained. This study confirmed high metabolic activity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right paratracheal and subcarinal lymph node stations. Crucially, the PET/CT did not demonstrate any FDG-avid lesions below the diaphragm or in the contralateral hemithorax, suggesting the absence of distant metastatic disease.

Given the potential for central nervous system involvement, a staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue acquisition. A transbronchial needle aspiration (TBNA) of a subcarinal lymph node (station 7) was performed. Pathologic evaluation of the specimen confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
The biopsy specimen was submitted for comprehensive molecular profiling and immunohistochemistry. The results are as follows:
- Histology: Adenocarcinoma, non-small cell type.
- PD-L1 Immunohistochemistry: Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) ≥50%.
- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) Panel: No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB): Intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 73-year-old presenting for initial evaluation of a newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IIIA (cT2bN2M0, AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup including PET/CT and brain MRI has ruled out distant metastatic disease. Molecular analysis reveals a high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%) without an identifiable targetable driver mutation. The patient is here to establish care and understand the diagnosis.
