**HPI:**
This is a 72-year-old individual with a past medical history of resected melanoma who is referred for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to primary care approximately one month ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and new-onset mid-back pain. The pain was described as constant and worsening with movement. An initial chest radiograph was abnormal, prompting further investigation.

A staging CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed which demonstrated a large mass in the right lower lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also revealed a destructive lesion in the thoracic spine and a distinct nodule in the left adrenal gland, concerning for metastatic disease. A staging MRI of the brain was subsequently obtained and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient reports ongoing back pain, which is partially controlled with scheduled analgesics. There is also mild fatigue, but appetite and weight have been stable. The patient remains fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living, with an estimated ECOG performance status of 1. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
- **CT Chest/Abdomen/Pelvis with contrast [Date de-identified]:** Revealed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right lower lobe. There was associated right hilar and subcarinal lymphadenopathy. Findings consistent with distant metastatic disease included a 2.5 cm left adrenal nodule and a lytic lesion involving the T7 vertebral body.
- **MRI Brain with and without contrast [Date de-identified]:** No evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.
- **CT-guided core needle biopsy, right lower lobe lung mass [Date de-identified]:** Pathology confirmed poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemical stains were positive for TTF-1 and Napsin-A, supporting a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing performed on the tumor tissue revealed the following:
- **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations/rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Negative (Tumor Proportion Score < 1%).
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Low (<2 mut/Mb).

1.  Metastatic (AJCC Stage IV) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Disease sites include the primary lung tumor, mediastinal lymph nodes, bone, and adrenal gland.
2.  Molecular profiling notable for no identified actionable driver mutations, negative PD-L1 expression, and low tumor mutational burden.
3.  ECOG Performance Status 1.
4.  History of resected melanoma.
