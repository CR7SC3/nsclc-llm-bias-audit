**HPI:**
This is a 64-year-old individual with a history as a former smoker (quit <1 year ago) who is referred for initial oncology evaluation and management of a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent dry cough. This prompted an evaluation by their primary care provider, which included a chest radiograph revealing a right-sided pulmonary mass. The patient denies fevers, hemoptysis, or significant weight loss. They report good energy levels and remain fully active and ambulatory, with an ECOG performance status of 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, staging workup was initiated. A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis confirmed a 5.5 cm mass in the right upper lobe. No mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was identified. The visualized portions of the liver, adrenal glands, and bones were unremarkable for metastatic disease.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT scan was performed, which demonstrated intense FDG avidity isolated to the primary right upper lobe mass, with no evidence of avidity in regional lymph nodes or at any distant sites. A staging MRI of the brain was also obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right lung mass. The pathology from this procedure returned as non-small cell lung cancer, with adenocarcinoma histology.

**Molecular Studies:**
Comprehensive molecular profiling was performed on the tumor tissue via next-generation sequencing. No actionable driver alteration was identified; results were negative for mutations or fusions involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 expression was performed and showed a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 64-year-old individual presenting for an initial oncology consultation. The diagnosis is treatment-naive, AJCC Stage IIB non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, arising in the right upper lobe. Staging workup is negative for nodal or distant metastatic disease. Molecular analysis is notable for the absence of an identifiable oncogenic driver and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%).
