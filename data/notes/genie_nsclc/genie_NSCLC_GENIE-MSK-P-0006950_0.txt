**HPI:**
This is a 61-year-old individual with a recent diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, referred for initial oncology consultation. The patient presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and mild dyspnea on exertion. An initial chest radiograph revealed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is a former smoker, having quit within the last year after a significant pack-year history. They report feeling generally well otherwise, with a good appetite and stable weight. Performance status is excellent; the patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also demonstrated bulky ipsilateral mediastinal lymphadenopathy, with the largest node measuring 2.8 cm in the right paratracheal station. No suspicious lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or other visualized abdominopelvic organs.

A subsequent PET/CT scan showed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right-sided mediastinal and hilar lymph nodes. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastases.

A staging MRI of the brain was performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for definitive diagnosis and staging. Fine-needle aspiration (FNA) was performed on the right paratracheal (4R) lymph node. Pathology from this specimen returned as poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tissue obtained from the EBUS-FNA.
-   **Actionable Driver Mutations:** An ALK gene rearrangement was detected.
-   **Other Markers:** Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **PD-L1 Immunohistochemistry:** PD-L1 expression was negative (Tumor Proportion Score < 1%).
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 61-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic Stage IIIA ALK-rearranged non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype. The patient is here for initial consultation to review the diagnosis and discuss the prognosis and management approach.
