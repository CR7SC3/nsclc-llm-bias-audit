**HPI:**
This is a 33-year-old who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker and was in their usual state of excellent health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially treated as a post-viral bronchitis without resolution. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained which revealed a right upper lobe pulmonary nodule. The patient denies any fevers, chills, night sweats, hemoptysis, or unintentional weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
The patient's initial diagnostic evaluation began with a chest X-ray which demonstrated a right upper lobe opacity.

A subsequent contrast-enhanced CT of the chest confirmed a 2.8 cm spiculated solid nodule in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

A PET/CT scan was performed for staging purposes. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the known right upper lobe primary tumor (SUVmax 11.2). There was no evidence of hypermetabolism in any mediastinal or hilar lymph nodes, nor were there any other sites of FDG-avid disease in the chest, abdomen, or pelvis to suggest distant metastatic disease.

A dedicated brain MRI with and without contrast was performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule was performed. Pathology returned as invasive adenocarcinoma.

Based on the above findings, the patient is staged as AJCC 8th Edition Stage IB (cT2a N0 M0).

**Molecular Studies:**
Pathology from the biopsy specimen confirmed non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Comprehensive next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alterations were identified. Specific testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 immunohistochemistry was not performed on the specimen.
