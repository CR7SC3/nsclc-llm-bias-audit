**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker who quit over one year ago. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and mild dyspnea on exertion. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider, who ordered initial imaging. The patient reports mild fatigue but is otherwise fully ambulatory and carrying out all activities of daily living. Performance status is ECOG 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, leading to further evaluation with a CT scan of the chest. The CT scan confirmed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, with associated mediastinal and right hilar lymphadenopathy.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was performed on [De-identified date]. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass. Avid uptake was also noted in multiple ipsilateral mediastinal lymph node stations, including the paratracheal (4R) and subcarinal (7) regions, consistent with N2 nodal disease. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastases.

A subsequent MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed for central nervous system staging and was negative for any intracranial metastatic disease.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis. A transbronchial needle aspiration (TBNA) of a right paratracheal (4R) lymph node was performed. Pathologic analysis of the specimen confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
The tumor tissue obtained via EBUS-TBNA was sent for next-generation sequencing. Molecular profiling did not identify any actionable driver alterations. Specifically, testing was negative for mutations, fusions, or amplifications in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested on this sample, consistent with standard practice at the time of tissue acquisition.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 55-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic, Stage IIIA (cT2aN2M0) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular studies are negative for targetable driver mutations. The patient is here today for an initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and review potential management options.
