**HPI:**
This is a 67-year-old individual with a past history of smoking who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. These symptoms did not resolve with a course of antibiotics prescribed by their primary care provider. Over the subsequent weeks, the patient also noted mild chest wall discomfort and an approximate 10-pound unintentional weight loss, which prompted further investigation.

The patient is currently functional with an ECOG performance status of 1, able to perform all self-care but restricted in physically strenuous activity. They deny any headaches, vision changes, focal weakness, significant bone pain, or abdominal discomfort.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung opacity, leading to a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest. This study characterized a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated bulky mediastinal and right-sided supraclavicular lymphadenopathy.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging purposes. I have personally reviewed this study, which demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass. Avid uptake was also noted in multiple lymph node stations, including ipsilateral hilar, contralateral mediastinal (station 4L), and right supraclavicular nodes, consistent with N3 nodal disease. No FDG-avid lesions suggestive of distant metastatic disease in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones were identified.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound-guided fine-needle aspiration (EBUS-FNA) of a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, with morphology consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the EBUS-FNA. No actionable driver alteration was identified; the tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was not tested, as this was not standard of care at the time of the tissue analysis. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 67-year-old former smoker who presents with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IIIB (cT2b N3 M0) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup confirms locally advanced disease with involvement of contralateral mediastinal and supraclavicular lymph nodes, without evidence of distant metastatic disease. Comprehensive molecular profiling of the tumor did not identify any actionable driver mutations.
