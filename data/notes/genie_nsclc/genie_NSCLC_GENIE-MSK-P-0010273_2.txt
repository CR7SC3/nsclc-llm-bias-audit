**HPI:**
This is a 70-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who presents for an initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider. The patient denies hemoptysis, chest pain, or significant shortness of breath. There has been no unintentional weight loss. The patient remains fully active and independent with all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, leading to further imaging. A staging PET/CT scan was performed on [De-identified]. This study confirmed a hypermetabolic 4.2 cm mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated FDG-avidity in ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymph nodes, concerning for regional metastatic involvement. No distant metastatic disease was identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was subsequently obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for definitive diagnosis and staging. Transbronchial needle aspiration was performed on the right upper lobe mass and a station 7 (subcarinal) lymph node. Pathology from both sites returned as adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.

**Molecular Studies:**
The tissue specimen was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry. No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was found to be negative for mutations or fusions involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, and NTRK. The tumor is also KRAS wild-type and ERBB2 (HER2) negative. PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was negative. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was low, at <2 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 70-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic, Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup is consistent with cT2b N2 M0 disease. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling is notable for the absence of any identifiable targetable driver alterations, negative PD-L1 expression, and low tumor mutational burden. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and potential management options.
