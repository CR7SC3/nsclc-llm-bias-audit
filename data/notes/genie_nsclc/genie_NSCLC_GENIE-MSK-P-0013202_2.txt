**HPI:**
This is a 68-year-old with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, presenting for initial oncology consultation. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately one month prior to evaluation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and new-onset headaches. Due to the persistence of symptoms, the patient presented to an outside facility for evaluation. An initial chest radiograph was concerning for a right-sided lung mass, which prompted a more extensive diagnostic workup. The patient has a past medical history of melanoma, treated with wide local excision years ago with no evidence of recurrence. The patient is a never-smoker and maintains an excellent performance status, continuing with daily activities without significant limitation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study confirmed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated a 2.5 cm left adrenal nodule and extensive mediastinal and contralateral supraclavicular lymphadenopathy, suspicious for metastatic disease.

Given the patient's report of headaches, a brain MRI with and without contrast was subsequently performed. This revealed multiple, small enhancing lesions in the bilateral cerebral and cerebellar hemispheres, consistent with metastatic deposits.

To obtain a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Histopathologic examination of the biopsy specimen revealed poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemical stains were positive for TTF-1 and Napsin-A, and negative for melanoma markers, confirming a primary lung adenocarcinoma.

The combination of these findings confirms a diagnosis of AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue.
- **Actionable driver mutation:** An ALK gene rearrangement was detected.
- **Other markers:** The tumor was negative for alterations in EGFR, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Low (<2 mut/Mb).
- **PD-L1:** Not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 68-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of metastatic (Stage IV) non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma. Staging studies confirmed a primary right upper lobe lung mass with metastatic disease to the brain, left adrenal gland, and distant lymph nodes. Molecular analysis of the tumor is notable for the presence of an ALK gene rearrangement. The patient is treatment-naive and is seen today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
