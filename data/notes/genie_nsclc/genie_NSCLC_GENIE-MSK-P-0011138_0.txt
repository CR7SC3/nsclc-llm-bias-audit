**HPI:**
This is a 53-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who is referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to their primary care provider approximately two months ago with a persistent, dry cough and progressive shortness of breath on exertion. An initial course of antibiotics for presumed bronchitis did not resolve the symptoms. The patient has otherwise maintained a good appetite and has had no significant weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0, with activities limited only by mild exertional dyspnea.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study characterized a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated extensive mediastinal and supraclavicular lymphadenopathy, including bulky contralateral mediastinal nodes consistent with N3 disease. The liver, adrenal glands, and visualized portions of the skeleton were unremarkable for metastatic disease.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in the extensive mediastinal and right supraclavicular nodal stations. The PET scan did not identify any sites of distant metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with contrast was also performed and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Histopathologic examination confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, with features of an adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue specimen.
-   **Actionable Driver Mutation:** An activating ERBB2 (HER2) exon 20 insertion was detected.
-   **Additional Markers:** The tumor was found to be negative for alterations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and KRAS.
-   **PD-L1:** PD-L1 expression was not tested, as this was not standard of care at the time of the tissue sequencing.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 53-year-old former smoker presenting for initial evaluation of a newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IIIB (cT2bN3M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for an activating ERBB2 exon 20 insertion. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis in detail.
