**HPI:**
This is a 73-year-old individual referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive mid-thoracic back pain. The pain was initially intermittent but became constant and was not fully relieved by over-the-counter analgesics. These symptoms prompted a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest radiograph revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity, leading to further imaging.

The patient reports associated fatigue and an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds over the past three months. They deny fevers, chills, night sweats, hemoptysis, or shortness of breath at rest. Neurologic review is negative for headache, vision changes, or focal weakness. Performance status is ECOG 1, fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care but restricted in physically strenuous activity by pain and fatigue.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study confirmed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Critically, the scan also identified a new lytic lesion in the T8 vertebral body, highly suspicious for a metastasis.

To complete staging and evaluate for intracranial disease, a brain MRI with contrast was performed and was negative for metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified Date]. Pathology confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Based on these findings, the patient is staged as having AJCC 8th Edition Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue specimen.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry (IHC) was negative, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of 0%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was low, at <2 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 73-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with a metastasis to bone. Molecular profiling is notable for the absence of any identified actionable driver alterations, negative PD-L1 expression, and low tumor mutational burden.
