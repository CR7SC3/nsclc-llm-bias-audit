**HPI:**
This is a 57-year-old individual with a significant smoking history who is referred for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a worsening cough, which had been chronic but recently increased in frequency and was associated with new-onset mild shortness of breath on exertion. The patient denies hemoptysis, chest pain, or significant weight loss. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise active, fully ambulatory, and maintains an excellent performance status, continuing with all activities of daily living.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest radiograph, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified date]. This study confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. No mediastinal, hilar, or supraclavicular lymphadenopathy was identified.

For staging, a whole-body PET/CT was performed on [De-identified date]. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the right upper lobe mass (SUV max 12.8). There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the mediastinal lymph nodes or at any distant sites, including the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A brain MRI with and without contrast was also completed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Histopathologic examination confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (NOS).

**Molecular Studies:**
Tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy was submitted for comprehensive molecular analysis.
- Next-generation sequencing for targetable alterations was performed. No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry (IHC) was strongly positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) analysis was performed and found to be high at >16 mutations/megabase.

1.  Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), not otherwise specified, of the right upper lobe.
2.  The clinical stage per AJCC 8th Edition is Stage IIA (cT2b N0 M0), based on imaging showing a 4.5 cm tumor with no nodal or distant metastatic involvement.
3.  Molecular profiling is notable for PD-L1 TPS ≥50% and a high Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB), with no actionable driver mutations identified.
4.  Current smoker with excellent performance status.
