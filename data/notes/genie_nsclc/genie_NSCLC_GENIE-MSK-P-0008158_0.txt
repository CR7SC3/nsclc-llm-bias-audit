**HPI:**
This is a 62-year-old with a history of tobacco use who presents for an initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray was concerning for a right-sided lung mass, and the patient was referred for further diagnostic evaluation. The patient reports a 10-pound unintentional weight loss over the past three months but otherwise feels well, with an ECOG performance status of 1. They are a current smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified]. This imaging revealed a 5.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. There was significant associated mediastinal and right supraclavicular lymphadenopathy, with the largest lymph node measuring 3.2 cm in the precarinal station. No suspicious lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was performed. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in extensive mediastinal and right supraclavicular lymph nodes, consistent with the prior CT findings. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease below the diaphragm or in the contralateral hemithorax to suggest distant metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathology confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical stage IIIB (cT3N3M0) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue from the biopsy specimen.
-   No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
-   PD-L1 expression was not tested, as is consistent with the standard of care at the time of the tissue sequencing.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 62-year-old patient with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, stage IIIB (cT3N3M0) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling is negative for common driver alterations. The patient is a current smoker with an excellent performance status (ECOG 1) and is here for initial evaluation and discussion.
