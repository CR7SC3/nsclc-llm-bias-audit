**HPI:**
This is a 67-year-old with a recent diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to an outside provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest radiograph was performed which demonstrated a small right upper lobe pulmonary nodule. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over one year ago, and otherwise reports a good baseline state of health with an excellent performance status (ECOG 0). The patient denies any fevers, chills, night sweats, hemoptysis, or unintentional weight loss. There are no focal neurologic symptoms such as headache, vision changes, or weakness. The patient is here today for initial medical oncology consultation to discuss the findings.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was obtained. This confirmed the presence of a 1.5 cm solid, spiculated nodule in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging purposes. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity localized to the known right upper lobe nodule. No other sites of abnormal hypermetabolism were identified in the thorax, abdomen, or pelvis to suggest nodal involvement or distant metastatic disease.

A staging MRI of the brain was performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung nodule was successfully performed. Pathology confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue.
- No actionable driver alterations were identified. The panel was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be high (>16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested on this specimen.

**Problem Summary:**
1.  Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), adenocarcinoma histology, AJCC Stage IA.
2.  Molecular profiling notable for high Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) with no identified actionable driver mutations.
3.  Excellent performance status, ECOG 0.
4.  Former smoker.
