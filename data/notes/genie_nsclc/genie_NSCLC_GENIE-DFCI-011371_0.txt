**HPI:**

The patient is a lifetime never-smoker and was in their usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially treated empirically with a course of antibiotics without improvement. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest radiograph was obtained which revealed a right upper lobe opacity. The patient was subsequently referred to pulmonology for further evaluation. The patient denies fevers, chills, night sweats, or significant weight loss. They maintain an excellent performance status and continue to work and engage in all usual activities.

On [De-identified], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.
