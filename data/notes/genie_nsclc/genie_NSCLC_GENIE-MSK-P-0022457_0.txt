**HPI:**
This is a 57-year-old individual with a history of former tobacco use who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately one month prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough and new-onset, localized pain in the right posterior chest wall. These symptoms prompted a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious opacity in the right upper lung. The patient is otherwise feeling well, with stable weight and appetite. Performance status is excellent; they remain fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, limited only by intermittent focal rib pain.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe with associated right hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also identified a destructive lesion in the right 7th rib and multiple hypodense lesions throughout the liver, suspicious for metastatic disease.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Histopathologic analysis confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, poorly differentiated.

A staging PET/CT scan was performed which demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, the mediastinal lymph nodes, the lytic lesion in the right 7th rib, and multiple hepatic lesions, confirming widespread metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was obtained and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tumor tissue from the biopsy was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing and PD-L1 analysis. Molecular profiling did not identify any actionable driver alterations; testing was negative for mutations or fusions involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was negative, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of 0%. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be high at 17 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
1.  Newly diagnosed Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastatic disease to bone and liver.
2.  Molecular profiling notable for no targetable driver mutations.
3.  PD-L1 negative (TPS 0%).
4.  Tumor Mutational Burden-High (TMB-H).
5.  ECOG Performance Status 1.
6.  Former smoker.
