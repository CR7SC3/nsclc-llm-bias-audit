**HPI:**
This is a 67-year-old with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, presenting for initial medical oncology consultation. The patient has a history as a former smoker (quit over one year ago). The patient initially presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and new-onset, moderately severe low back pain. An initial course of antibiotics for presumed bronchitis did not provide relief. Due to the persistence of symptoms, particularly the focal back pain, the patient was referred for further imaging studies which initiated the diagnostic workup. The patient remains ambulatory with an ECOG performance status of 1, limited primarily by back discomfort, but is otherwise managing all activities of daily living.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A chest X-ray was first obtained, which revealed a suspicious opacity in the right upper lung. This prompted a follow-up CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast. This study confirmed the presence of a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also identified bulky mediastinal and right hilar lymphadenopathy. Critically, evidence of distant metastatic disease was identified, including a 2.5 cm mass in the left adrenal gland and a lytic lesion within the T8 vertebral body, corresponding to the patient's area of back pain. A few small nodules in the contralateral (left) lung were also noted, suspicious for additional metastatic sites.

For definitive diagnosis and staging, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathology from this specimen returned as non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (NOS).

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed as part of the standard workup for metastatic NSCLC and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the lung biopsy was sent for comprehensive molecular profiling via next-generation sequencing (NGS) and for PD-L1 immunohistochemistry. The results are as follows:

-   An actionable driver alteration was detected: **MET amplification (high-level)**.
-   The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in **EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2**.
-   PD-L1 expression was high, with a **Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%**.
-   Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was **high at >16 mutations/megabase**.
