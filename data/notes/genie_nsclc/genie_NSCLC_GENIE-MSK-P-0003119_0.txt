**HPI:**
This is a 51-year-old with a history of smoking (quit within the last year) who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive fatigue. This was initially treated as bronchitis without improvement. Due to worsening dyspnea on exertion and an unintentional 10-pound weight loss, the patient sought further evaluation. A chest X-ray revealed a left-sided lung opacity, prompting a follow-up CT scan. The patient currently reports mild shortness of breath with significant activity but is otherwise fully ambulatory and managing all activities of daily living. Performance status is ECOG 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A comprehensive diagnostic evaluation was undertaken following the initial abnormal imaging.

- **CT Chest, Abdomen, and Pelvis with contrast on [De-identified Date]:** This study identified a 5.2 cm spiculated mass in the left upper lobe with associated bulky mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. Multiple suspicious lesions were noted consistent with distant metastatic disease, including a 3.1 cm hypodense lesion in the right hepatic lobe and several non-regional, subcarinal lymph nodes. No adrenal or osseous metastases were definitively identified.

- **MRI Brain with and without contrast on [De-identified Date]:** Performed for staging of metastatic disease. This study was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

- **Interventional Radiology-Guided Biopsy on [De-identified Date]:** A core needle biopsy of the right hepatic lobe lesion was performed. Pathology from this tissue confirmed metastatic adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemical staining was positive for TTF-1 and Napsin-A, supporting a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Comprehensive molecular profiling was performed on the liver biopsy tissue via next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry (IHC). The results are as follows:

- **Histology:** Adenocarcinoma, consistent with lung primary.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Positive, Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) ≥50%.
- **Actionable Driver Mutations:**
  - **MET amplification (high-level) detected.**
- **Other Biomarkers:**
  - **Negative for mutations/rearrangements in:** *EGFR*, *ALK*, *ROS1*, *BRAF*, *MET* exon 14 skipping, *RET*, *NTRK*, *KRAS*, *ERBB2 (HER2)*.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (9 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 51-year-old with a new diagnosis of AJCC Stage IV metastatic non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. The disease is metastatic to the liver and distant lymph nodes. Molecular analysis of the tumor is notable for high-level MET amplification and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The patient has a good performance status and is here today to discuss the diagnosis and the implications of these findings.
