**HPI:**
This is a 53-year-old who presents for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. Over the past month, they also noted the onset of new, constant mid-thoracic back pain. An initial chest X-ray was abnormal, prompting further investigation. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over a year ago. They remain fully ambulatory and independent in all activities of daily living with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed which identified a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also revealed multiple small nodules in the contralateral left lung, a 2.1 cm left adrenal nodule, and a suspicious lytic lesion in the thoracic spine.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was obtained for staging. This confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right lung mass, mediastinal nodes, the contralateral pulmonary nodules, the left adrenal lesion, and an osseous lesion in the T9 vertebral body, consistent with widespread metastatic disease.

A staging MRI of the brain was performed and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified]. Pathology confirmed poorly differentiated non-small cell carcinoma with features most consistent with adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) of the tumor tissue was performed. No actionable driver alterations were identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 expression was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of 1-49%. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2-16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 53-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging studies confirm metastatic disease involving the contralateral lung, adrenal gland, and bone. Molecular profiling is negative for targetable driver alterations but is positive for PD-L1 expression (TPS 1-49%). The patient presents today for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and establish a plan for care.
