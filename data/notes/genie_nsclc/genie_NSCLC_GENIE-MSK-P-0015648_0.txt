**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 65-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit within the last year) who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer.

These symptoms did not resolve with a course of antibiotics prescribed by their primary care provider. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained, which revealed a suspicious right-sided lung mass.
