**HPI:**
This is a 48-year-old individual referred to the Thoracic Oncology clinic for initial consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months ago when they began to experience a persistent dry cough and a new sensation of fatigue. When symptoms did not resolve, they presented to their primary care physician. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung opacity, prompting further evaluation. The patient is a former smoker with a significant pack-year history, having quit within the last year. They report a good performance status, with activity limited only by occasional dyspnea on significant exertion. They deny any fevers, chills, night sweats, hemoptysis, or significant unintentional weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed. This study characterized a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, associated with several enlarged ipsilateral hilar lymph nodes.

To establish a tissue diagnosis and complete staging, the patient underwent a PET/CT scan. This revealed high FDG avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in a right hilar lymph node. Crucially, the PET/CT did not demonstrate any FDG-avid lesions in the mediastinum, contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or skeleton, suggesting disease confined to the right hemithorax.

Subsequently, the patient underwent a bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) and biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic analysis of the biopsy specimen confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

The combination of these findings is consistent with a clinical Stage IIB (cT2bN1M0) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the diagnostic biopsy was sent for molecular analysis. Results available to date show a low tumor mutational burden (TMB) of <2 mutations/megabase. PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was not tested. Comprehensive next-generation sequencing profiling to assess for genomic alterations including, but not limited to, EGFR, ALK, and ROS1, is currently underway and results are pending.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 48-year-old former smoker presenting for initial management discussion of a newly diagnosed Stage IIB (cT2bN1M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, arising in the right upper lobe. The diagnostic workup has confirmed disease localized to the lung and ipsilateral hilar nodes, with no evidence of distant metastatic spread. Initial molecular testing is notable for a low TMB, with further genomic results pending.
