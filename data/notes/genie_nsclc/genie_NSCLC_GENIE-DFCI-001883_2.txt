**HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS**
This is a 57-year-old individual with a past medical history significant for ovarian cancer, now in remission, who is referred for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to an outside facility approximately six weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest radiograph demonstrated a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise well, reporting no fevers, chills, hemoptysis, or unintentional weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP**
Staging workup was initiated. A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study identified a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed prominent ipsilateral mediastinal lymphadenopathy, including enlarged paratracheal and subcarinal nodes, with the largest measuring 2.2 cm in short-axis diameter. No suspicious lesions were identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or other visualized intra-abdominal organs.

A subsequent PET/CT scan on [De-identified Date] confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right-sided mediastinal nodal stations corresponding to the CT findings. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere to suggest distant metastases.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was completed on [De-identified Date] and was negative for intracranial metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed. Pathology returned invasive adenocarcinoma, moderately differentiated.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC 8th Edition Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer.

**MOLECULAR STUDIES**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue from the lung biopsy. The following results were reported:
- **Actionable Driver:** An activating EGFR point mutation, L858R in exon 21, was detected.
- **Other Markers:** The tumor was found to be negative for ALK rearrangement, ROS1 rearrangement, BRAF V600E mutation, MET exon 14 skipping mutation, RET fusion, NTRK fusion, KRAS mutation, and ERBB2 (HER2) mutation.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- **PD-L1:** PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**PROBLEM SUMMARY**
This is a 57-year-old never-smoker with an excellent performance status who presents with a new diagnosis of Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation. The patient is treatment-naive for this malignancy. The clinical situation was discussed in detail.
