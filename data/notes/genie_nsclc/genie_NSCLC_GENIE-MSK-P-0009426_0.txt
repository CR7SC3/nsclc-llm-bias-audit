**HPI:**
This is a 59-year-old individual referred for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a current smoker and presented to their primary care provider approximately two months ago with a persistent, dry cough and progressive shortness of breath with exertion. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung mass, prompting further investigation. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, though notes fatigue. The history was obtained from the patient and a review of outside records.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which identified a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated extensive and bulky mediastinal and ipsilateral hilar lymphadenopathy, consistent with significant nodal involvement. No suspicious hepatic, adrenal, or other distant visceral lesions were identified.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and the extensive mediastinal nodal stations. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease at any distant sites to suggest metastatic spread.

For definitive central nervous system staging, a brain MRI with and without contrast was performed, which was negative for any intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified]. Pathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue.
- No actionable driver alterations were identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- A loss-of-function mutation was detected in the STK11 gene.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 59-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic Stage IIIB non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling is notable for an STK11 loss-of-function mutation with no other identified targetable driver alterations. The patient is here for initial consultation and discussion of the diagnosis.
