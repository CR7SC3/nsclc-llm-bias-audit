**HPI:**
This is a 76-year-old patient with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who is referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to their primary care provider with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided pulmonary nodule, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise in their usual state of health, with an excellent performance status. They deny constitutional symptoms such as fevers, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a diagnostic CT of the chest was performed. This confirmed a solitary 2.1 cm solid nodule in the right upper lobe. No mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was identified.

For staging, a whole-body PET/CT was subsequently obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity localized to the known right upper lobe nodule. There was no evidence of FDG-avid lymphadenopathy or distant metastatic disease. A contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was also performed as part of the complete staging evaluation and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule on [De-identified date]. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma of the lung.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue obtained from the biopsy was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry. Key findings are as follows:
- **Actionable driver mutation:** Positive for a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion.
- **Other drivers:** Negative for fusions/mutations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **PD-L1:** Negative (Tumor Proportion Score <1%).
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 76-year-old patient with an excellent performance status presenting for initial evaluation of a new, treatment-naive diagnosis of Stage IA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup, including PET/CT and brain MRI, confirms disease is localized to a solitary right upper lobe nodule without evidence of nodal or distant metastatic spread. Molecular testing is notable for the presence of a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion.
