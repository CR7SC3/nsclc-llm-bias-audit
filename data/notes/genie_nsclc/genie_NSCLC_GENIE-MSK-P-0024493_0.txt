**HPI:**
This is a 51-year-old individual who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation to discuss a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker and was in their usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough. This was initially attributed to a viral illness but did not resolve. Over the subsequent weeks, the patient also noted mild, intermittent shortness of breath with exertion. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, leading to further diagnostic evaluation. The patient has no significant constitutional symptoms such as fevers, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed following the abnormal chest X-ray. This study confirmed the presence of a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. No mediastinal, hilar, or other regional lymphadenopathy was identified. There was no evidence of metastatic disease in the visualized portions of the abdomen or pelvis.

For definitive staging, a whole-body PET/CT scan was obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity isolated to the right upper lobe mass. No other sites of abnormal FDG uptake were seen, specifically no hypermetabolic lymph nodes or distant metastatic lesions. A brain MRI with and without contrast was also performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic review of the tissue specimens confirmed the diagnosis of lung adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the biopsy was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry (IHC) for treatment planning. The results are as follows:
-   **Actionable driver:** An activating ERBB2 (HER2) exon 20 insertion was detected.
-   **Other drivers:** Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and KRAS.
-   **PD-L1 Expression:** PD-L1 IHC was negative, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of <1%.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 51-year-old never-smoker presenting with a new diagnosis of Stage IIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype, of the right upper lobe. The workup is negative for nodal or distant metastatic disease. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for an activating ERBB2 exon 20 insertion mutation and negative PD-L1 expression. The patient is treatment-naive and is seen today for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis.
