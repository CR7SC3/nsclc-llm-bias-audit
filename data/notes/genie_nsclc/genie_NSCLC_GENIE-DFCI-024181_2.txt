**HPI:**
This is a 66-year-old individual with a history of being a former smoker (quit >1 year ago) who presents for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to diagnosis, when a persistent, non-productive cough developed. This was later associated with mild dyspnea on exertion, which prompted a primary care evaluation. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, with an ECOG performance status of 1. Past medical history is notable for a prior treated melanoma and a separate, prior non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is treatment-naive for the current diagnosis.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
The initial workup began with a chest radiograph that revealed a right-sided lung mass. A subsequent contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified], which characterized a 4.5 cm primary mass in the right upper lobe with associated bulky mediastinal and contralateral hilar lymphadenopathy. No suspicious hepatic, adrenal, or osseous lesions were identified.

A PET/CT scan was performed for staging purposes on [De-identified]. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in multiple mediastinal and contralateral hilar nodal stations, consistent with locoregionally advanced disease. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease at any distant sites to suggest metastasis. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A definitive tissue diagnosis was obtained via endobronchial ultrasound-guided (EBUS) fine-needle aspiration of a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic analysis of the specimen confirmed adenocarcinoma of the lung. Based on the integrated findings of the imaging and pathology, the patient is diagnosed with locally advanced, unresectable disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry (IHC) were performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the EBUS biopsy. The results are as follows:
- Histology: Adenocarcinoma, non-small cell type.
- Actionable Mutation: KRAS G12C mutation detected.
- Additional Mutations: STK11 loss-of-function mutation detected.
- Negative for other actionable drivers: EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and ERBB2 mutations were not detected.
- PD-L1 Expression: Negative, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of <1%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB): Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
