**HPI:**
This is a 69-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit more than one year ago) who presents for initial oncology consultation to discuss a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent dry cough and a sense of chest tightness. This was initially managed symptomatically, but due to lack of resolution, a chest X-ray was obtained which showed a right-sided lung mass. The patient denies hemoptysis, fevers, or significant pain, but does endorse a 10-pound unintentional weight loss over the past three months. The patient remains fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic CT scan of the chest confirmed the presence of a 4.8 cm mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated enlarged ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes, concerning for regional metastasis.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT scan was performed on [De-identified date]. This study demonstrated intense FDG uptake in the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in multiple ipsilateral mediastinal lymph node stations (paratracheal and subcarinal), consistent with nodal involvement. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the contralateral lung, pleura, or at any distant sites such as the liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A subsequent brain MRI with contrast was also performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the primary right upper lobe mass was performed. Histopathologic examination of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, squamous cell carcinoma subtype.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor biopsy specimen. No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was found to be negative for mutations or rearrangements involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was not tested, as this was not standard of care at the time the tissue was originally profiled. The tumor mutational burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate at 14 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
1.  Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), squamous histology, of the right upper lobe.
2.  Clinical stage is AJCC Stage IIIA (non-metastatic), based on locoregional involvement of the primary tumor and ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes, with no evidence of distant metastatic disease.
3.  Tumor molecular profile is negative for known actionable driver mutations. Tumor mutational burden is intermediate.
4.  ECOG performance status 1.
