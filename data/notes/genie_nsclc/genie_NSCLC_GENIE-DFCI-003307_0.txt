**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A CT scan of the chest was performed which confirmed the presence of a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also revealed ipsilateral hilar lymphadenopathy.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the primary right upper lobe mass and the right hilar lymph nodes. Crucially, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the contralateral hemithorax, mediastinum, or at any distant sites such as the liver, adrenal glands, or bones, consistent with locoregionally confined disease.

A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, with features of an adenocarcinoma histology.

**MOLECULAR STUDIES:**
