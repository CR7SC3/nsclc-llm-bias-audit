**HPI:**
This is a 76-year-old individual with a history of former tobacco use who is seen today for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care provider approximately two months ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive exertional dyspnea. This was associated with mild fatigue and an unintentional weight loss of approximately 15 pounds over the preceding three months. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care but has limited strenuous activity due to shortness of breath (ECOG performance status 1). The initial clinical evaluation prompted a diagnostic imaging workup.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest radiograph revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity, leading to a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis on [De-identified date]. This study identified a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right lower lobe. There was extensive ipsilateral hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Critically for staging, the scan also demonstrated an enlarged contralateral supraclavicular lymph node measuring 2.1 cm, consistent with distant metastatic disease. No metastatic lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was performed for comprehensive staging. This confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right lower lobe mass, multiple mediastinal nodal stations, and the contralateral supraclavicular lymph node, confirming distant disease. No other sites of FDG-avid disease were identified.

A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified date], the patient underwent an ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the right lower lobe pulmonary mass. Pathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the biopsy specimen was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry. The results are as follows:
- **Histology:** Invasive Adenocarcinoma, non-small cell type.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Positive, Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) 1-49%.
- **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- **Other Genomic Alterations:** A loss-of-function mutation was detected in the STK11 gene.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 76-year-old individual presenting with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, metastatic to distant lymph nodes. The patient is treatment-naive with a good performance status. Molecular profiling is notable for PD-L1 expression in the 1-49% range and an STK11 loss-of-function mutation, with no identified actionable driver mutations. The diagnosis, stage, and implications of these findings have been discussed at length with the patient.
