**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed which demonstrated a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. The scan also confirmed a moderate-to-large right-sided pleural effusion with associated nodular pleural thickening, suspicious for metastatic involvement. There was no evidence of distant metastatic disease in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A dedicated MRI of the brain was performed for staging and was negative for intracranial metastases.

To obtain a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided thoracentesis. Cytological analysis of the pleural fluid was positive for malignant cells, and immunohistochemical staining was consistent with a primary lung adenocarcinoma.
