**HPI:**
This is a 68-year-old individual with a history of being a never-smoker, referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately one month prior to presentation, when they began to experience progressive shortness of breath on exertion and a persistent, dry cough. This prompted a visit to their primary care physician. The patient denies hemoptysis, fevers, or significant weight loss but does report some mild, non-focal right-sided chest discomfort. Performance status is excellent; the patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, with an ECOG of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided pleural effusion and a suspicious right upper lobe lung mass. A subsequent staging CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified date]. This confirmed a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, associated with mediastinal lymphadenopathy. A large, complex right-sided pleural effusion was also identified. There was no evidence of distant metastatic disease in the abdomen or pelvis.

On [De-identified date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathology from this specimen returned as invasive adenocarcinoma.

To address the symptomatic dyspnea and to aid in staging, the patient underwent a right-sided thoracentesis. Cytologic analysis of the pleural fluid was positive for malignant cells, consistent with metastatic adenocarcinoma, establishing M1a disease.

A contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was performed as part of the standard staging workup and revealed no evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry (IHC) were performed on the lung biopsy tissue.
- An actionable driver mutation was detected: **EGFR L858R point mutation**.
- The following biomarkers were **negative** for pathogenic alterations: ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by IHC was **negative** (Tumor Proportion Score <1%).
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was **intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb)**.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 68-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The disease is characterized by a primary right upper lobe mass with mediastinal lymphadenopathy and a malignant pleural effusion (M1a). Molecular profiling is notable for the presence of a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation and negative PD-L1 expression. The patient is treatment-naive and is seen today for initial consultation and to discuss the diagnosis.
