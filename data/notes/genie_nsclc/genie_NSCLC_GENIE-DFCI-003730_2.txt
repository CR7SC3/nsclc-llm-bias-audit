**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which identified a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. This was associated with extensive mediastinal and contralateral hilar lymphadenopathy, consistent with locoregionally advanced disease.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in multiple mediastinal and contralateral nodal stations, confirming N3 disease. Importantly, the PET/CT demonstrated no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere, with no suspicious lesions in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones to suggest distant metastasis.

To complete staging and rule out central nervous system involvement, a dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was obtained. This study was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis. A fine-needle aspiration was performed on a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed metastatic adenocarcinoma with features consistent with a lung primary.
