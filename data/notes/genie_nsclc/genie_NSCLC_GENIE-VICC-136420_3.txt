**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A comprehensive staging evaluation was undertaken.

A CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast identified a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed multiple enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes, several lytic lesions in the thoracic spine, and multiple hypodense lesions in the liver, all suspicious for metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan confirmed high FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe lung mass, mediastinal lymph nodes, a T7 vertebral body, and at least three distinct liver lesions, consistent with widespread metastatic disease.

An MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed for central nervous system staging and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

An interventional radiology-guided core needle biopsy of one of the accessible liver lesions was performed. Pathology confirmed a diagnosis of moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma. The morphology and immunohistochemical profile were consistent with a lung primary.

Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.
