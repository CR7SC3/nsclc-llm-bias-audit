**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 67-year-old individual with a past medical history notable for melanoma, presenting for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until a persistent dry cough prompted a chest X-ray, which revealed a solitary pulmonary nodule. The patient is a former smoker, having quit more than one year ago. They report an excellent functional status, remaining fully active without limitations, and deny any constitutional symptoms including fevers, chills, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. ECOG performance status is 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed, which confirmed the presence of a 2.2 cm spiculated solid nodule in the right upper lobe. No other pulmonary nodules or significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy were identified.

For staging purposes, a PET/CT scan was obtained on [De-identified Date]. This study demonstrated intense FDG avidity corresponding to the right upper lobe nodule, with no evidence of hypermetabolic activity in the mediastinum, contralateral lung, or any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule was subsequently performed. Pathologic examination of the biopsy specimen confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IA non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing performed on the tumor tissue revealed the following:
-   **Actionable driver detected:** EGFR L858R point mutation (sensitizing)
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb)
-   **Negative for rearrangements/mutations in:** ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, ERBB2
-   **PD-L1:** Not tested

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 67-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, clinical Stage IA (cT1bN0M0) non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma of the right upper lobe. The patient has an excellent performance status of ECOG 0. Molecular profiling of the tumor is significant for the presence of a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation.
