The patient denies fevers, chills, or significant weight loss but does endorse fatigue. An initial chest radiograph was abnormal, prompting further investigation.

The patient is a former smoker with a significant pack-year history, having quit less than one year ago. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care but is limited in strenuous activity due to dyspnea, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 1. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis in detail.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Histopathologic examination confirmed a diagnosis of poorly differentiated non-small cell carcinoma, with immunohistochemical staining consistent with squamous cell carcinoma.
