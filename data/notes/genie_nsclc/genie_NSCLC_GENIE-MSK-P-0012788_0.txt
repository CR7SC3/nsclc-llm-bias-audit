**HPI:**
This is a 69-year-old who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough. Due to the persistence of symptoms and a history as a former smoker (quit >1 year ago), they were evaluated by their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe lung opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient reports good energy levels and has an excellent performance status (ECOG 0). They deny any hemoptysis, chest pain, fevers, or unintentional weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed. This study characterized a 3.8 cm spiculated solid mass in the right upper lobe. No mediastinal, hilar, or supraclavicular lymphadenopathy was identified.

For definitive staging, a whole-body PET/CT was obtained on [De-identified]. This scan demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe mass. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in any regional lymph nodes or at any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease. A brain MRI with and without contrast was also performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) performed on the tumor tissue identified a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion. The tumor was negative for alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 69-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, Stage IIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling is notable for the presence of a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion.
