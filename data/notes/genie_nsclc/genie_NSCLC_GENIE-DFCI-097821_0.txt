**HPI:**
This is a 49-year-old individual with an excellent performance status (ECOG 0) who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker and was in a usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and a mild sensation of chest fullness. These symptoms prompted a visit to their primary care provider, who ordered a chest radiograph that revealed a right upper lobe lung mass. The patient was subsequently referred for further diagnostic evaluation. The patient denies fevers, chills, night sweats, or significant weight loss. They remain fully active, working, and engaging in all usual activities of daily living without limitation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This study characterized a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed extensive mediastinal and supraclavicular lymphadenopathy, concerning for N3 nodal disease.

To complete staging, a PET/CT scan was obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in multiple contralateral mediastinal and right supraclavicular lymph nodes, consistent with the nodal disease seen on CT. Importantly, the PET/CT showed no evidence of distant metastatic disease. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue acquisition. A fine-needle aspiration (FNA) was performed on a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic analysis of the biopsy specimen confirmed the diagnosis.

**Molecular Studies:**
Pathology from the EBUS-FNA of the mediastinal lymph node confirmed non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology.
Comprehensive next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue.
- An actionable driver was identified: **ALK gene rearrangement**.
- Testing was negative for alterations in EGFR, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 expression showed a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of **≥50%**.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be **intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb)**.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 49-year-old never-smoker presenting with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IIIB (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype. The diagnosis is based on a primary right upper lobe tumor with extensive N3 nodal involvement and no evidence of distant metastatic disease. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the presence of an ALK rearrangement and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The patient has an excellent performance status.
