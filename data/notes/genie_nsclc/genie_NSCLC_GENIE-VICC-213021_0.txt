**HPI:**
This is a 51-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive fatigue. An initial course of antibiotics from their primary care provider did not resolve the symptoms. Due to the persistence of the cough, a chest X-ray was obtained which showed a right upper lobe opacity, prompting a follow-up CT scan of the chest. The patient reports no significant weight loss, hemoptysis, or bone pain. They remain fully ambulatory and independent with activities of daily living, though they note shortness of breath with moderate exertion. ECOG performance status is 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT chest with contrast revealed a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. This was associated with bulky ipsilateral mediastinal (4R) and subcarinal (station 7) lymphadenopathy.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT was performed for staging. This confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and the noted mediastinal and subcarinal lymph nodes. No FDG-avid lesions suggestive of distant metastatic disease in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bone were identified.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent flexible bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis and definitive nodal staging. Transbronchial needle aspiration was performed on the right upper lobe mass and the subcarinal lymph node. Pathology from both sites confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained via biopsy.
- An actionable driver mutation was detected: EGFR exon 19 deletion (sensitizing).
- The panel was negative for other alterations, including ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 51-year-old former smoker with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, non-metastatic Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma, positive for a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion.
