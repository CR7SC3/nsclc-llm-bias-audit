**HPI:**
This is the initial medical oncology consultation for a 67-year-old with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient has a history of former tobacco use, having quit more than one year ago. They presented to primary care approximately six weeks ago with a persistent dry cough. Over the past month, they developed progressive right-sided hip pain, which now makes ambulation more difficult. An associated 10-pound unintentional weight loss over the last three months is reported. The patient remains active with daily activities but notes increased fatigue. Performance status is ECOG 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
The patient's initial workup began with a chest radiograph for the persistent cough, which demonstrated a right upper lobe opacity. A subsequent contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed for staging. This study confirmed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, with associated ipsilateral mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. A lytic lesion concerning for metastasis was noted in the right iliac bone.

A whole-body PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained, which demonstrated intense hypermetabolic activity in the primary right lung mass, the mediastinal lymph nodes, and a solitary focus of high uptake in the right iliac bone, confirming distant metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed as part of the standard staging workup and revealed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

A definitive diagnosis was established via a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic review confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue specimen.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified.
- Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was strongly positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 67-year-old with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma, with a primary site in the right lung and a metastasis to bone. The patient is treatment-naive with an ECOG performance status of 1. Molecular studies are notable for the absence of an identifiable driver alteration and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The patient is seen in consultation today to discuss the new diagnosis and establish oncologic care.
