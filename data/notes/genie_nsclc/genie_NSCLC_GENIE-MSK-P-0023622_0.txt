**HPI:**
This is a 72-year-old individual referred for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest x-ray revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is a former smoker, having quit more than a year ago. Despite the diagnosis, the patient reports feeling well, maintaining their weight and appetite, and has an excellent performance status (ECOG 0), continuing with all usual activities of daily living without restriction.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This demonstrated a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also revealed extensive and bulky mediastinal and right hilar lymphadenopathy, concerning for significant nodal involvement. No suspicious lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or other visceral organs.

A PET/CT was subsequently performed for staging purposes. This study confirmed high FDG avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in the extensive mediastinal and hilar lymph node stations. Importantly, the PET/CT showed no evidence of FDG-avid disease at any distant sites.

A dedicated brain MRI with contrast was also completed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

To obtain a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) and fine-needle aspiration (FNA) of a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the EBUS-FNA. The results are as follows:
- Actionable driver mutation detected: KRAS G12C
- No pathogenic mutations were identified in: EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested on this sample.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 72-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic, Stage IIIB (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. The disease is characterized by a KRAS G12C mutation. The patient is currently asymptomatic with an excellent performance status.
