**HPI:**
This is an initial oncology consultation for a 71-year-old with a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of good health until approximately six weeks ago when a pre-operative chest radiograph for an elective orthopedic procedure revealed a right upper lobe pulmonary nodule. This prompted further investigation. The patient is a current smoker but is otherwise active with an ECOG performance status of 0. The patient denies any specific cancer-related symptoms, including hemoptysis, chest pain, shortness of breath, or constitutional symptoms such as fevers or significant weight loss. A review of systems is otherwise unremarkable. The patient is here today to discuss the findings and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a diagnostic CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified Date]. This confirmed the presence of a 1.8 cm solid, spiculated nodule in the right upper lobe. No other suspicious pulmonary nodules or significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy were identified.

To complete staging, a PET/CT was subsequently performed. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the right upper lobe nodule. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the mediastinum, contralateral lung, or any distant sites to suggest metastatic involvement. A staging MRI of the brain was also completed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided fine-needle aspiration of the right upper lobe nodule. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed a diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy. The results revealed an activating KRAS G12C mutation. Testing was negative for targetable alterations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and ERBB2. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested on this specimen.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 71-year-old with an excellent performance status who presents for evaluation of a newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IA (AJCC 8th Edition) KRAS G12C-mutant non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma. The diagnosis was made during the workup of an incidentally discovered solitary pulmonary nodule. Comprehensive staging evaluation has confirmed the disease is localized to the right upper lobe without evidence of regional lymph node or distant metastatic spread.
