**HPI:**
This is a 60-year-old individual with a history of current tobacco use who presents for initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. Over the subsequent weeks, the patient also began to experience intermittent headaches and a sense of imbalance, which prompted a visit to an urgent care center on [De-identified].

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest CT scan performed at the time of the urgent care visit revealed a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated bulky mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. Given the concurrent neurologic symptoms, a contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was obtained, which demonstrated multiple parenchymal lesions consistent with metastatic disease.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was performed for staging, which confirmed high FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and the mediastinal lymph nodes. No other sites of FDG-avid distant metastatic disease were identified outside of the brain.

The patient was referred for an interventional pulmonology consultation and underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS)-guided biopsy of a mediastinal lymph node. Pathology from this tissue confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue. The key findings are as follows:
- An actionable driver mutation was detected in KRAS G12C.
- No pathogenic mutations were identified in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, or ERBB2.
- A loss-of-function mutation was identified in STK11.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1–49% range.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 60-year-old with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastases to the brain. Molecular profiling is notable for a KRAS G12C mutation, PD-L1 expression of 1-49%, and a co-occurring STK11 loss-of-function mutation. The patient is treatment-naive and presents today for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
