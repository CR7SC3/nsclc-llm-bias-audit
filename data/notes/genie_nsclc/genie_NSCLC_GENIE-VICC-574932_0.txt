**HPI:**
This is a 50-year-old individual with a past medical history notable only for well-controlled hypertension, who is referred for initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they began to develop a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. Over the subsequent weeks, these symptoms were accompanied by the onset of a dull, aching pain in the right posterior ribs and a 10-pound unintentional weight loss. Initial evaluation with a primary care provider led to a chest X-ray that demonstrated a right-sided lung opacity and a moderate pleural effusion. The patient was subsequently referred for further diagnostic workup. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, but is restricted in physically strenuous activity due to dyspnea (ECOG Performance Status 1).

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A staging CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study revealed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. There was an associated moderate right-sided malignant-appearing pleural effusion with nodular pleural thickening. Multiple sclerotic and lytic osseous lesions were identified in the thoracic spine and right-sided ribs, consistent with metastatic disease. No metastatic lesions were identified in the liver or adrenal glands. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was subsequently performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed by Interventional Radiology on [De-identified Date]. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, with an immunohistochemical profile consistent with adenocarcinoma histology.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue specimen.
- An actionable driver mutation was identified: **MET exon 14 skipping mutation**.
- The panel was negative for mutations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as **high** at >16 mut/Mb.
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 50-year-old never-smoker who presents for initial evaluation of newly diagnosed, treatment-naive **Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer (adenocarcinoma)**, with metastatic involvement of the bone and pleura. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for a MET exon 14 skipping mutation and high tumor mutational burden. The patient is seen today to discuss the implications of this diagnosis and to review potential management strategies.
