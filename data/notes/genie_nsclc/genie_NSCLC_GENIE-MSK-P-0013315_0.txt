**HPI:**
This is a 53-year-old individual with a history of being a former smoker (quit over one year ago) who is referred for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to this consultation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough. This did not resolve with a course of antibiotics. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained which demonstrated a right-sided pulmonary opacity. The patient is otherwise feeling well, with no reports of hemoptysis, chest pain, dyspnea, or constitutional symptoms such as fevers or weight loss. They maintain an excellent performance status (ECOG 0), continuing with all usual daily activities without restriction.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed a 2.8 cm solid, spiculated nodule in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal, hilar, or supraclavicular lymphadenopathy. No suspicious lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or other visualized portions of the abdomen and pelvis.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was performed. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the known right upper lobe nodule (SUVmax 10.2). No other sites of abnormal FDG uptake concerning for nodal or distant metastatic disease were identified. A staging brain MRI with and without contrast was also obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule was subsequently performed. Pathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of lung adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
The biopsy specimen was submitted for comprehensive molecular profiling via next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry (IHC).
- **Actionable driver mutation:** An EGFR L858R point mutation was detected.
- **Other drivers:** Testing was negative for ALK rearrangement, ROS1 rearrangement, BRAF V600E mutation, MET exon 14 skipping mutation, RET fusion, NTRK fusion, KRAS mutation, and ERBB2 (HER2) mutation.
- **Immunotherapy biomarkers:**
  - PD-L1 expression by IHC was negative (Tumor Proportion Score <1%).
  - Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 53-year-old with an excellent performance status who presents with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, AJCC Stage IB non-small cell lung cancer (adenocarcinoma histology). The workup is consistent with localized disease confined to the lung. Molecular testing is notable for a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation.
