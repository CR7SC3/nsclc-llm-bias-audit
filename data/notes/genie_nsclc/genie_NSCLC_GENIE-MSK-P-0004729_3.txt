**HPI:**
This is a 78-year-old with a past medical history of melanoma, presenting for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over a year ago.

The patient initially presented to an outside emergency department approximately four weeks ago with complaints of a persistent, non-productive cough, progressive dyspnea on exertion, and new-onset headaches. Due to the neurologic symptoms, a CT of the head was performed which was suspicious for metastatic lesions. This prompted a full staging workup. The patient reports a 10-pound unintentional weight loss over the past three months and increasing fatigue, but remains ambulatory and capable of all self-care, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 1. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
- **CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast** revealed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. The scan also demonstrated multiple bilateral pulmonary nodules consistent with contralateral metastatic disease, several hypodense lesions in the liver suspicious for metastases, and multiple lytic lesions in the thoracic spine and pelvis. Extensive mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy was also noted.

- **MRI of the brain with and without contrast** was performed to evaluate the findings from the head CT and confirmed the presence of multiple, scattered intraparenchymal metastatic lesions with associated vasogenic edema.

- **CT-guided core needle biopsy** of a right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified]. Pathology returned confirming a primary lung adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
- **Histology:** Adenocarcinoma.
- **Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) of tumor tissue:**
  - **Actionable driver mutation detected:** EGFR L858R point mutation.
  - **Negative for rearrangements/mutations in:** ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, ERBB2.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- **PD-L1 expression:** Not performed, as testing was not standard at the time of initial sequencing.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 78-year-old with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging studies confirm a heavy burden of metastatic disease involving the brain, contralateral lung, liver, and bone. Molecular profiling of the tumor is positive for a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation. The patient has a good performance status (ECOG 1) but is symptomatic from the disease.
