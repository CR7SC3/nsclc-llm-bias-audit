**History of Present Illness**

This is a 73-year-old individual referred for consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they noted the onset of a persistent, non-productive cough and mild dyspnea on exertion. These symptoms prompted a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung opacity, leading to further cross-sectional imaging.

The patient reports their activity level has decreased slightly due to shortness of breath but they remain fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care. They deny any fevers, night sweats, hemoptysis, or bone pain. Appetite is fair with no significant weight loss reported. The patient is a former smoker, having quit more than one year ago. The current ECOG performance status is 1.

**Diagnostic Workup**

A diagnostic CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed. This imaging identified a primary mass in the right upper lobe of the lung, along with prominent ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy. No suspicious lesions were identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones.

To complete staging, a PET/CT scan was obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe lung mass and in the right-sided mediastinal lymph nodes, consistent with the CT findings. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastases. A dedicated MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis and staging. A fine-needle aspiration biopsy was successfully performed on a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic review of this specimen confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung cancer primary.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) NSCLC.

**Molecular Studies**

Tumor tissue from the biopsy was submitted for next-generation sequencing analysis. The key findings are as follows:
*   **Actionable Driver Mutations:** A sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion was detected.
*   **Other Genes:** Testing was negative for genomic alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
*   **PD-L1 Expression:** Not tested.
