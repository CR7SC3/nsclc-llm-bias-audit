**Diagnostic Workup:**
Initial evaluation by the patient's primary care physician included a chest X-ray, which revealed a suspicious mass in the lung. This finding was further investigated with a CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis, which confirmed a primary lung mass, along with evidence of regional lymphadenopathy and several suspicious distant lesions. A PET-CT scan was also completed as part of the staging workup, demonstrating hypermetabolic activity in the primary lung lesion, involved mediastinal lymph nodes, and several distant sites, including the previously identified brain lesions. Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, AJCC Stage IV, with brain metastases. The patient is treatment-naive, and this represents the first oncology evaluation.
