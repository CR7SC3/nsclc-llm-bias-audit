This was followed by the insidious onset of right-sided chest wall pain, described as a constant, dull ache, and progressive fatigue. **DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which confirmed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Additionally, it confirmed multiple hypermetabolic lesions in the liver as well as a new focus of intense uptake in a right-sided rib, corresponding to the patient's area of pain. These findings were highly concerning for widespread metastatic disease. A staging MRI of the brain was performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.
