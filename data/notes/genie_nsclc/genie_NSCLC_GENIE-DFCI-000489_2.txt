**HPI:**
This is a 78-year-old who presents for an initial oncology consultation. The patient has a significant smoking history, having quit within the last year, and was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation. The patient developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion, accompanied by a 15-pound unintentional weight loss. An initial chest radiograph performed by the primary care physician was suspicious for a right-sided lung mass, prompting further imaging. The patient is otherwise active and independent with activities of daily living.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified]. This revealed a large, spiculated 5.2 cm mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also identified multiple suspicious lesions, including a 1.8 cm nodule in the contralateral left lower lobe, a 3.4 cm left adrenal mass, and a lytic lesion in the T7 vertebral body.

A subsequent PET/CT scan confirmed high FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, the contralateral lung nodule, the left adrenal mass, and the T7 vertebral body, consistent with widespread metastatic disease. A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed as part of the staging evaluation and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass was performed on [De-identified]. Pathology returned poorly differentiated squamous cell carcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alteration was identified; testing was negative for EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested, as this was not standard of care at the time of diagnosis.

**Problem Summary:**
1. Metastatic (Stage IV) non-small cell lung cancer, squamous histology.
2. Sites of metastatic disease include the contralateral lung, left adrenal gland, and bone.
3. No targetable driver mutations identified on molecular profiling.
4. History of tobacco use.
5. ECOG performance status 1.
