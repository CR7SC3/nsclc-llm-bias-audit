**HPI:**
This is a 61-year-old with a significant smoking history who presents for initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was followed by mild, progressive dyspnea on exertion and an unintentional 10-pound weight loss over the past two months. They initially presented to their primary care physician, who ordered a chest X-ray that was suspicious for a lung mass. The patient remains fully ambulatory and independent in all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 1. They continue to smoke.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A follow-up contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the left upper lobe. The scan also revealed bulky, conglomerate mediastinal and left supraclavicular lymphadenopathy, concerning for extensive nodal involvement. No suspicious lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones to suggest distant metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary left upper lobe mass as well as in multiple mediastinal (stations 4L, 7) and left supraclavicular (N3) nodal stations. The PET scan did not reveal any other sites of FDG-avid disease consistent with distant metastases. A staging MRI of the brain was also obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis. A fine-needle aspiration (FNA) of a subcarinal (station 7) lymph node was performed. Pathology from this specimen confirmed poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the EBUS-FNA.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression was not tested on the specimen.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 61-year-old individual with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, non-metastatic Stage IIIB (cT2bN3M0) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Comprehensive molecular profiling is negative for targetable driver alterations. The patient presents today for initial consultation to review the diagnosis and discuss the goals of care and management options.
