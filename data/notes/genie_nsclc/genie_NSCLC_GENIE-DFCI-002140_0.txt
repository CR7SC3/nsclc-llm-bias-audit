**HPI:**
This is a 73-year-old individual with a history of being a never-smoker who presents for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately six weeks prior to presentation, when they noted the insidious onset of progressive dyspnea on exertion and a persistent, non-productive cough. These symptoms gradually worsened, prompting an evaluation by their primary care provider. The patient remains fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 1. They deny fevers, chills, or significant weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung mass and a moderate pleural effusion. A subsequent contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis confirmed a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, along with a large, right-sided pleural effusion and multiple pleural nodules. No other sites of distant thoracic or abdominopelvic metastatic disease were identified.

A PET/CT scan was performed for staging, which demonstrated intense FDG avidity in the right upper lobe primary mass and widespread avidity throughout the right pleura, consistent with malignant pleural involvement. No other suspicious hypermetabolic foci were seen in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A dedicated MRI of the brain with contrast was obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided thoracentesis for both diagnostic and therapeutic purposes. Cytological analysis of the pleural fluid confirmed the presence of malignant cells consistent with adenocarcinoma. The diagnosis is therefore metastatic non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
The pleural fluid cell block was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing. Results are notable for an activating EGFR L858R point mutation. Additionally, high-level MET amplification was detected. The tumor was negative for alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was not tested. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 73-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (cT2a N0 M1a) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, metastatic to the pleura. Molecular profiling is notable for a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation with co-occurring high-level MET amplification. The patient is treatment-naive and presents for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis.
