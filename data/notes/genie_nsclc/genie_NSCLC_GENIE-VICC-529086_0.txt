**HPI:**
This is a 58-year-old individual with a history of former tobacco use (quit >1 year ago) who presents for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, at which time they developed a persistent, dry cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. This prompted an evaluation with their primary care provider, leading to initial imaging studies which were abnormal. The patient has an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, which led to a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest. This study characterized a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, along with associated ipsilateral hilar and peribronchial lymphadenopathy.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was performed on [De-identified Date]. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the primary right upper lobe mass as well as the right hilar lymph nodes. Crucially, no FDG-avid lesions were identified in the mediastinum, contralateral hemithorax, or in any distant visceral or osseous sites to suggest metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was subsequently obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

To secure a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue specimen. No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 expression was performed, revealing a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of <1% (negative). Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) analysis was also conducted and was found to be high, at >16 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 58-year-old who presents for initial consultation with a new diagnosis of Stage IIB non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup, including PET/CT and brain MRI, has confirmed localized disease without evidence of distant metastases. Comprehensive molecular profiling is notable for the absence of targetable driver mutations, a PD-L1 TPS of <1%, and a high tumor mutational burden. The patient is treatment-naive and is here to discuss the diagnosis and establish a plan for management.
