**HPI:**
This is an initial medical oncology consultation for a 60-year-old with a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker, having quit within the past year. The patient initially presented to an outside facility several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest radiograph was notable for a right upper lobe opacity, which prompted further investigation with a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest. The patient is otherwise feeling well, with no reports of hemoptysis, dyspnea, chest pain, or constitutional symptoms such as fevers or weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0. The patient has a remote history of another malignancy, which has been in remission for many years. The patient is now referred for evaluation and to discuss management options.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic CT of the chest performed on [De-identified date] confirmed the presence of a 3.2 cm spiculated solid nodule in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. There was no associated mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

For purposes of staging, a whole-body PET/CT was performed on [De-identified date]. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the known right upper lobe mass. Crucially, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in any regional lymph node stations or at any distant sites.

A contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was obtained on [De-identified date] and revealed no evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass on [De-identified date]. Histopathologic analysis of the specimen confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma. Based on the comprehensive workup, the patient is staged as having clinical Stage IB (cT2aN0M0) disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
- An actionable driver mutation was detected in **KRAS G12C**.
- The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be **intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb)**.
- PD-L1 expression was **not tested**.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 60-year-old, treatment-naive former smoker with an excellent performance status who presents with a new diagnosis of Stage IB (cT2aN0M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, located in the right upper lobe. Molecular testing of the tumor is notable for the presence of a KRAS G12C mutation. The patient is seen today for initial consultation to review these findings.
