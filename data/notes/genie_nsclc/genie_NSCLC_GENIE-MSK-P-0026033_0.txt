**HPI:**
This is a 59-year-old individual with no significant past medical history who presents for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a new, persistent, dry cough. This was followed by the onset of intermittent headaches and a dull, aching pain in the right hip. Due to the progression of these symptoms, the patient presented to their primary care provider.

An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung mass, prompting further investigation. The patient has experienced an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds over the past three months and notes increasing fatigue but remains fully ambulatory and capable of self-care. They deny fevers, chills, or hemoptysis.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study identified a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also revealed a 2.1 cm hypodense lesion in the right hepatic lobe and a lytic lesion in the right iliac bone, both suspicious for metastatic disease.

Given the neurologic symptoms, an MRI of the brain with and without contrast was obtained. This demonstrated multiple, small, ring-enhancing intracranial lesions scattered throughout the cerebral hemispheres, consistent with metastatic deposits.

A subsequent PET/CT scan confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe lung mass, mediastinal nodes, the right hepatic lesion, and the right iliac bone lesion, confirming widespread metastatic disease.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass by Interventional Radiology. Pathology returned as poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue.
- **Actionable driver mutation detected:** EGFR L858R point mutation.
- **Negative for rearrangements or mutations in:** ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 expression:** Not tested.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Low (<2 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 59-year-old never-smoker presenting for initial evaluation of newly diagnosed Stage IV NSCLC, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastases to the brain, bone, and liver. Molecular profiling is notable for a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation. The patient is treatment-naive.
