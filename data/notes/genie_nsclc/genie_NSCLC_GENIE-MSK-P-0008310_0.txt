**HPI:**
This is a 63-year-old individual with a history of being a never-smoker, who is seen in consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately six weeks prior to presentation, when they began to experience progressive shortness of breath on exertion and a persistent, non-productive cough. Symptoms worsened, limiting their ability to perform daily activities, which prompted an evaluation with their primary care provider. The patient denies fevers, chills, or recent infectious symptoms. There has been an associated unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds over the past two months.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung opacity and a large pleural effusion. The patient was subsequently sent for a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis for staging. This study confirmed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, associated with a large, loculated right-sided pleural effusion. There was no evidence of other distant metastatic disease in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A dedicated MRI of the brain with contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

Given the large symptomatic pleural effusion, the patient underwent a video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery (VATS) for drainage and pleural biopsy on [De-identified Date]. Approximately 1.5 liters of serosanguinous fluid were drained, which resulted in significant improvement of the patient's dyspnea. Pathological analysis of the pleural tissue biopsies confirmed the diagnosis.

**Molecular Studies:**
Pathology from the pleural biopsy confirmed non-small cell lung cancer with adenocarcinoma histology. Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue.

-   **Actionable Mutations:** A sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion was detected.
-   **Other Markers:** The tumor was negative for rearrangements or mutations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **PD-L1:** PD-L1 expression was not tested.
-   **TMB:** Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was intermediate (reported as 2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 63-year-old never-smoker who presents with a new diagnosis of AJCC Stage IV (M1a) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype, with metastasis to the pleura. The patient's performance status is good, though previously limited by dyspnea which has now improved following therapeutic thoracentesis. Molecular profiling of the tumor is critically important and reveals a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion. The patient is here today for initial oncology consultation to discuss the diagnosis in detail.
