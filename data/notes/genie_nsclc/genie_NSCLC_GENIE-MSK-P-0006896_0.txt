**HPI:**
This is an initial medical oncology consultation for a 67-year-old with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker who quit over one year ago. The patient initially presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and new-onset mid-thoracic back pain. An initial chest X-ray was abnormal, prompting further imaging. The patient reports mild fatigue but is otherwise maintaining weight and appetite. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 1, with the patient continuing to manage all activities of daily living and light household tasks without difficulty. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic workup was initiated following the abnormal chest X-ray.

Imaging:
- CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast on [De-identified Date] revealed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Multiple sub-centimeter nodules were noted in the contralateral (left) lung. A lytic lesion was identified in the T7 vertebral body, concerning for a metastasis.
- A whole-body PET/CT scan on [De-identified Date] confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, mediastinal lymph nodes, the contralateral pulmonary nodules, and the T7 vertebral body lesion. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified.
- A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for staging and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

Pathology:
- A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified Date]. Histopathologic examination confirmed invasive, moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue.
- Actionable driver detected: MET amplification (high-level).
- Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 67-year-old former smoker with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive Stage IV (cT2b N2 M1b) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastatic disease to the contralateral lung and bone. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for high-level MET amplification.
