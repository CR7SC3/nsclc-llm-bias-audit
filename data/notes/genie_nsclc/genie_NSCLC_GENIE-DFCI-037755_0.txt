**HPI:**
This is a 57-year-old with a history of current tobacco use who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider, who ordered initial imaging. The patient is otherwise active and independent with all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 0. They deny any fevers, chills, night sweats, hemoptysis, or unintentional weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe pulmonary nodule. This was followed by a diagnostic CT of the chest with contrast, which confirmed the presence of a 2.2 cm solid, spiculated nodule in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. No significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was noted.

For staging purposes, a PET/CT scan was performed on [De-identified]. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe nodule. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the mediastinal lymph nodes, contralateral lung, or any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was subsequently performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule. Pathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
-   **Actionable driver(s) detected:** EGFR L858R point mutation (sensitizing).
-   **Negative for:** ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, ERBB2.
-   **PD-L1 Expression:** Positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of 1–49%.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 57-year-old who presents with a new diagnosis of Stage IA non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, based on the AJCC 8th edition staging system. The tumor is characterized by a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation. Comprehensive staging workup is negative for any regional nodal involvement or distant metastatic disease.
