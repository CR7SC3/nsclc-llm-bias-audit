**HPI:**
This is an 82-year-old who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately six weeks prior to evaluation, when they noted the onset of a persistent, dry cough and the appearance of a new, firm, non-tender lump on their anterior chest wall. They also report some mild fatigue but deny fevers, chills, significant weight loss, or hemoptysis. Due to these concerns, they presented to their primary care provider for evaluation. The patient has a remote smoking history, having quit less than one year ago. They remain fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung mass, which prompted further imaging. A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study identified a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe with associated mediastinal adenopathy. In addition, the scan revealed a 1.5 cm nodule in the contralateral left lower lobe and a 2.2 cm enhancing subcutaneous nodule in the right anterior chest wall, consistent with the patient's palpable finding. These findings were highly suspicious for metastatic malignancy. A staging MRI of the brain was subsequently performed and was negative for any intracranial metastatic disease.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent an ultrasound-guided core needle biopsy of the accessible subcutaneous chest wall nodule. Pathology from this specimen confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue specimen. The key findings are as follows:
-   **Actionable Driver:** MET amplification (high-level) detected.
-   **Negative for:** EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, ERBB2 mutations/rearrangements.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
-   **PD-L1:** Not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is an 82-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer (adenocarcinoma histology), with metastatic disease involving the contralateral lung and subcutaneous tissue. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for a high-level MET amplification. The patient is seen today to discuss these findings in detail.
