**HPI:**
This is a 37-year-old who presents for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive mid-back pain. The pain was initially managed with over-the-counter analgesics but worsened, prompting a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe lung mass. The patient is a former smoker, having quit approximately 5 years ago after a 10-pack-year history. They are otherwise active and fully ambulatory, though report increased fatigue and some pain related to their symptoms. Review of systems is otherwise negative.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, associated with mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also revealed multiple hypoattenuating lesions in the liver, suspicious for metastatic disease.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This demonstrated intense FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, mediastinal lymph nodes, multiple hepatic lesions, and a focus of uptake in the L3 vertebral body, confirming metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified]. Pathology returned adenocarcinoma of the lung.

A staging MRI of the brain with contrast was performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified.
- Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by IHC was negative (Tumor Proportion Score <1%).
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was low at <2 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 37-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging studies confirm metastatic disease to the liver and bone. Brain MRI is negative for metastatic disease. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the absence of any actionable driver alterations, negative PD-L1 expression, and low TMB. The patient has an excellent performance status (ECOG 1) and is now seen for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and management options.
