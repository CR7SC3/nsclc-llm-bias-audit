**HPI:**
This is a 70-year-old individual with a history of being a never-smoker who is referred for initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially managed with over-the-counter remedies without relief. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained which revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity. The patient denies any associated hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or significant weight loss. Performance status is excellent; the patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study confirmed the presence of a 4.3 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

For definitive diagnosis and staging, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass on [De-identified Date]. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Subsequent staging evaluation included a PET/CT scan performed on [De-identified Date]. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the known right upper lobe primary tumor. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in any regional lymph nodes or at any distant sites. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with localized disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor biopsy specimen. No actionable driver alteration was identified; testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 expression was performed, and the result was negative. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was assessed and found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 70-year-old never-smoker who presents for initial evaluation of a new diagnosis of AJCC Stage IIA non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup has confirmed disease localized to the right upper lobe without evidence of nodal or distant metastatic spread. Molecular profiling is notable for the absence of any identified targetable driver mutations and negative PD-L1 expression. The patient is here today to discuss this new diagnosis and establish care.
