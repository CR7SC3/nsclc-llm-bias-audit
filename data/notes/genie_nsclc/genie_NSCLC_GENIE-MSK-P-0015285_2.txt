**HPI:**
This is a 51-year-old individual with a history of current tobacco use who presents for an initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive shortness of breath on exertion. This was associated with mild chest discomfort and an unintentional 10-pound weight loss. These symptoms prompted a visit to their primary care provider, which led to a diagnostic workup.

An initial chest radiograph revealed a right-sided lung mass, prompting a follow-up contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis. This study confirmed the presence of a large mass in the right upper lobe with associated bulky mediastinal lymphadenopathy. There was no evidence of distant visceral or osseous metastatic disease on the body CT. A staging PET/CT was subsequently performed, which demonstrated high FDG avidity in the primary right lung mass and in ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes, consistent with locoregional involvement. No other sites of FDG-avid disease suggestive of distant metastases were identified. A brain MRI with contrast was also obtained as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient was referred to pulmonology and underwent a bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for definitive diagnosis and staging. Fine-needle aspiration was performed on both the primary mass and a right paratracheal lymph node. The patient has tolerated the diagnostic procedures well and remains ambulatory with a good performance status, though reports ongoing fatigue and dyspnea with significant exertion.

**Pathology & Molecular Studies:**
- **Histology:** Biopsy of the right paratracheal lymph node confirmed poorly differentiated Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer, Not Otherwise Specified (NSCLC, NOS).
- **Molecular Profiling:** Comprehensive next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue.
    - No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
    - **PD-L1:** Immunohistochemistry was negative (Tumor Proportion Score < 1%).
    - **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was high, measured at >16 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 51-year-old who presents with a new diagnosis of locally advanced, unresectable Stage IIIA (cT2bN2M0) non-small cell lung cancer, NOS histology. The patient is a current smoker and has an excellent performance status. Staging workup including PET/CT and brain MRI has confirmed disease confined to the right hemithorax. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the absence of targetable driver mutations, negative PD-L1 expression, and a high tumor mutational burden. The patient is here for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and review potential management options.
