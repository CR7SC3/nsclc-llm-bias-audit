**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 51-year-old individual referred for initial consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. A chest X-ray was obtained which revealed a right upper lobe lung nodule, prompting further investigation.

The patient is a former smoker, having quit over one year ago. They report feeling generally well otherwise, with no significant weight loss, fevers, or night sweats. Energy levels are good, and they maintain an ECOG performance status of 0.

A diagnostic workup was initiated. A CT scan of the chest confirmed a solid, spiculated nodule in the right upper lobe.
