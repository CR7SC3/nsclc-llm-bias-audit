**HPI:**
This is a 66-year-old with a history of smoking (quit <1 year ago) who presents for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and noted an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider, who ordered a chest radiograph that showed a right-sided lung opacity. The patient has remained fully ambulatory and self-caring throughout this period, with an estimated ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified]. This study revealed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated bulky mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. The abdominal portion of the scan was notable for a solitary 2.8 cm hypodense lesion in the right hepatic lobe, suspicious for a metastasis.

A subsequent PET/CT scan on [De-identified] confirmed high FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe lung mass (SUV max 14.2), the mediastinal lymph nodes (SUV max 9.8), and the solitary liver lesion (SUV max 8.5), consistent with metastatic malignancy. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified. A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for staging and was negative for intracranial metastases.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core biopsy of the accessible liver lesion on [De-identified]. Pathology from this procedure returned as poorly differentiated carcinoma with squamous features, consistent with a metastasis from a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the liver biopsy tissue.
- No actionable driver alterations were identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 66-year-old former smoker who is treatment-naive, presenting for initial evaluation of a new diagnosis of Stage IV (cT2b N2 M1b) non-small cell lung cancer of squamous cell histology, metastatic to the liver. Staging is notable for the absence of brain metastases. Molecular profiling did not identify any actionable driver mutations. The patient is here to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
