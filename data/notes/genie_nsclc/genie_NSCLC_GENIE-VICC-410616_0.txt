**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 70-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who is referred for initial oncology evaluation of a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to their primary care provider approximately six weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and new-onset, localized back pain. An initial chest radiograph revealed a right-sided lung opacity, prompting further investigation.

The patient reports associated fatigue but has maintained their weight and appetite. Performance status is good, estimated at ECOG 1, as they remain fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, though limited in strenuous activity by their symptoms. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This study identified a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated ipsilateral mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also revealed a lytic lesion in a thoracic vertebral body, suspicious for a bone metastasis.

To further delineate the extent of disease, a whole-body PET/CT scan was obtained. This confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, the mediastinal lymph nodes, and the previously noted vertebral body lesion, consistent with metastatic disease. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified.

For central nervous system staging, an MRI of the brain with and without contrast was completed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed. Pathology review of the tissue specimen confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the lung mass biopsy was sent for comprehensive molecular profiling and immunohistochemistry.
- **PD-L1:** Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) is positive in the 1-49% range.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Low (<2 mut/Mb).
- **Next-Generation Sequencing:** No actionable driver alterations were identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
