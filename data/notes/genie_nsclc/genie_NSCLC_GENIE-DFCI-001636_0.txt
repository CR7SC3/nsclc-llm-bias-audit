**REASON FOR CONSULTATION:** New diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer.

**HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:**
This is a 75-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who presents for initial oncologic evaluation. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially treated with a course of antibiotics by their primary care provider without resolution.

Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained which revealed a right upper lobe opacity. This prompted a follow-up contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, which confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated bulky ipsilateral mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. There was no evidence of distant visceral or contralateral pulmonary disease on the chest CT.

A comprehensive staging workup was initiated. A PET/CT scan demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right hilar and paratracheal lymph node stations, consistent with the findings on CT. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastases. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for any intracranial metastatic disease.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. The patient tolerated the procedure well. Pathology confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is now referred to medical oncology for discussion of findings and to consider treatment options. The patient is active and independent with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
*   **CT Chest/Abdomen/Pelvis:** Revealed a 4.5 cm right upper lobe lung mass with associated right hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy. No distant metastatic disease identified in the chest, abdomen, or pelvis.
*   **PET/CT:** Showed hypermetabolic activity corresponding to the right upper lobe mass and right-sided hilar and mediastinal lymph nodes. No evidence of distant metastatic disease.
*   **MRI Brain:** Negative for intracranial metastases.
*   **Pathology (CT-guided biopsy, right lung mass):** Non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (NOS).

**MOLECULAR STUDIES:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor specimen.
*   No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for alterations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   PD-L1 expression was not tested.
*   Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was reported as high (>16 mut/Mb).

**PROBLEM SUMMARY:**
This is a 75-year-old former smoker who presents with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic, Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, histology NOS. Molecular profiling is notable for a high tumor mutational burden but is negative for any identified actionable driver mutations.
