**HPI:**
This is a 63-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit <1 year ago) who is referred for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately six weeks prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. Symptoms worsened to include mild pleuritic-type right-sided chest pain, which prompted an evaluation with their primary care physician. An initial chest X-ray was abnormal, leading to further cross-sectional imaging. The patient maintains a good performance status (ECOG 1) but is limited by shortness of breath with significant exertion. They deny fevers, chills, or significant weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A staging CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed on [De-identified Date]. This imaging revealed a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung, associated with several enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes. Critically, there was a large, loculated right-sided pleural effusion. There was no evidence of metastatic disease in the abdomen or pelvis. A subsequent MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided thoracentesis for both diagnostic and therapeutic purposes. Cytological analysis of the pleural fluid confirmed the presence of malignant cells. The pathology from the fluid cell block was consistent with adenocarcinoma, confirming a lung primary.

Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV disease (cT2a N2 M1a), with the malignant pleural effusion constituting M1a disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the pleural fluid specimen. The key findings are as follows:
-   **Actionable driver mutation:** A sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion was detected.
-   **Other drivers:** Testing was negative for alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
-   **PD-L1:** This was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 63-year-old, treatment-naive individual presenting for initial consultation with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The disease is characterized by a primary right upper lobe mass with mediastinal nodal involvement and a malignant pleural effusion. Molecular testing is notable for a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion. The patient is seen today to review these findings and discuss the implications of this diagnosis.
