**HPI:**
This is an initial oncology consultation for a 55-year-old who presents for evaluation and management of newly diagnosed non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over one year ago. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. This was associated with mild fatigue and an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds over three months. The patient denies hemoptysis, fevers, or significant chest pain. Due to the persistence of symptoms, they sought evaluation with their primary care provider. An initial chest x-ray was concerning for a right upper lobe lung mass, prompting further diagnostic workup. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date], which confirmed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also demonstrated a moderate right-sided pleural effusion and a solitary, 2.8 cm hypodense lesion in the right lobe of the liver, suspicious for metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan on [De-identified Date] demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe lung mass, as well as in the right pleura and the previously identified liver lesion, consistent with widespread metastatic disease. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic review confirmed invasive, poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma of the lung. The presence of malignant pleural effusion and a liver lesion radiographically consistent with metastasis establishes a diagnosis of Stage IV disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tumor tissue was sent for comprehensive molecular profiling.
- PD-L1 expression by tumor proportion score (TPS) is 35% (positive, 1-49% range).
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) is intermediate at 9 mutations/megabase.
- Next-generation sequencing did not identify any actionable driver alterations. Specific genes tested and found to be negative include: EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14 skipping, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2 (HER2).
- A loss-of-function mutation was detected in the KEAP1 gene.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 55-year-old with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (cT2b N0 M1b) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology, with metastases to the pleura and liver. Molecular testing is notable for PD-L1 expression of 35% and a KEAP1 loss-of-function mutation, with no actionable driver mutations identified. The patient is treatment-naive and presents today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
