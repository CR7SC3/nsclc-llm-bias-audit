**HPI:**
This is an initial medical oncology consultation for a 62-year-old individual with a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care provider approximately three months ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is a former smoker, having quit within the past year. They report good baseline health, with an excellent performance status (ECOG 0), and deny constitutional symptoms such as fevers, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. They have maintained their appetite and energy levels.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified], which identified a 4 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. No definitive mediastinal or hilar adenopathy was noted. The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathology confirmed invasive, moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma.

Staging evaluation was completed with a PET/CT scan, which demonstrated high FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass. There was no evidence of FDG-avid mediastinal lymphadenopathy or distant metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain was also performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any intracranial metastases. This comprehensive evaluation is consistent with a clinical AJCC Stage IIA (cT2bN0M0) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
- Somatic molecular profiling: No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was found to be negative for mutations or fusions involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 Expression: Positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB): Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

In summary, this is a 62-year-old with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic Stage IIA non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling is notable for high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%) but is negative for targetable driver alterations.
