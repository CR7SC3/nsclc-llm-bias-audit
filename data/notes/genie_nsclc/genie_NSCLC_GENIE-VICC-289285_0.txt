**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 76-year-old individual referred for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately six weeks ago when they noted a new, firm, non-tender lump on their anterior chest wall. This lump gradually increased in size, prompting a visit to their primary care provider. The patient denies any associated cough, shortness of breath, hemoptysis, or constitutional symptoms such as fevers or significant weight loss. They remain fully active and independent with all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 0. Of note, the patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

Initial evaluation included a chest CT scan which identified a primary lung mass in addition to the subcutaneous lesion. A subsequent PET/CT was performed for staging, which confirmed the findings and led to a biopsy of the most accessible lesion. The patient presents today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
**Imaging:**
*   **CT Chest/Abdomen/Pelvis with contrast:** Revealed a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the left upper lobe. A 2.5 cm enhancing subcutaneous nodule was noted in the right anterior chest wall, corresponding to the palpable area of concern. No other suspicious visceral or nodal disease was identified in the chest or abdomen.
*   **PET/CT:** Demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the left upper lobe lung mass (SUVmax 12.4) and the right chest wall subcutaneous nodule (SUVmax 9.8). No other sites of FDG-avid metastatic disease were identified.
*   **MRI Brain:** Performed for staging, this was negative for intracranial metastases.

**Pathology:**
*   **Excisional biopsy, right chest wall subcutaneous nodule:** The specimen showed infiltration by a poorly differentiated malignant neoplasm. Immunohistochemical stains were positive for p40 and CK5/6, and negative for TTF-1 and Napsin-A, confirming a diagnosis of metastatic squamous cell carcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (tissue-based) was performed on the biopsy specimen.
*   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   **PD-L1 Expression:** PD-L1 by immunohistochemistry (IHC) was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1–49% range.
*   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was determined to be high, at >16 mutations/megabase.
