**HPI:**
This is a 58-year-old individual who presents for an initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive right-sided back pain. The pain was initially managed with over-the-counter analgesics with minimal relief. Due to worsening symptoms and the development of fatigue, the patient sought evaluation. An initial chest X-ray was concerning for a right upper lobe mass, which prompted further imaging. The patient is a current smoker. They report a good performance status (ECOG 1), remaining ambulatory and capable of all self-care, though activities are limited by pain.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified date], which confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. The scan also revealed multiple enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes and a lytic lesion in the T7 vertebral body, suspicious for metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe mass, mediastinal lymph nodes, and the previously noted T7 vertebral lesion, consistent with metastatic malignancy. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified.

As part of the initial staging workup for advanced NSCLC, a brain MRI with contrast was obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified date]. Pathology returned adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alteration was identified. Specific testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 58-year-old current smoker who presents with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, Stage IV (cT2b N2 M1b) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology, with metastasis to bone. Molecular profiling did not identify an actionable driver mutation.
