**HPI:**
This is the initial medical oncology consultation for a 50-year-old individual referred for management of newly diagnosed metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to an outside facility approximately one month ago with a several-week history of persistent, dull headaches and a mild, non-productive cough. Due to the neurologic symptoms, an initial brain MRI was performed which raised concern for metastatic disease. The patient was subsequently referred for a full diagnostic evaluation. The patient remains fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care but reports increased fatigue over the past month. Performance status is ECOG 1. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the initial brain imaging, a comprehensive staging workup was completed. A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis identified a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, associated with bulky mediastinal and right hilar lymphadenopathy. No metastatic disease was identified in the abdomen or pelvis.

A PET/CT scan confirmed high FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe primary mass, as well as in multiple mediastinal nodal stations. The scan also demonstrated multiple FDG-avid foci in the brain, corresponding to the lesions seen on MRI, with no other sites of distant metastatic disease identified.

An MRI of the brain with contrast confirmed the presence of multiple scattered enhancing lesions in the cerebral and cerebellar hemispheres, consistent with metastatic deposits.

The diagnosis was established via a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic analysis confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the lung biopsy. The key findings are as follows:
- An actionable sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion was detected.
- Testing was negative for alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested, as the sequencing was performed on a panel from a pre-2017 platform where this was not standard.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 50-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastases to the brain at the time of presentation. The patient has a good performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for an activating EGFR exon 19 deletion. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish oncologic care.
