**HPI:**
This is a 73-year-old with a history of being a never-smoker who presents for initial oncology consultation. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider, who obtained a chest radiograph revealing a right upper lobe opacity. The patient was subsequently referred for further diagnostic evaluation. The patient denies fevers, chills, night sweats, or significant weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which confirmed the presence of a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also identified several enlarged ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was obtained. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in multiple ipsilateral mediastinal lymph node stations (4R, 7), consistent with locoregional nodal metastases. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the contralateral chest, liver, adrenal glands, bones, or other distant sites to suggest M1 disease.

Given the staging findings, a contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was performed to complete the workup, which was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed by Interventional Radiology. Pathologic review of the tissue confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma of the lung.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue specimen.
- **Actionable Driver Mutations:** A ROS1 gene rearrangement was detected.
- **Other Drivers:** Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- **PD-L1:** Not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 73-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, characterized by a ROS1 rearrangement. The disease is confined to the right lung and ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes, with no evidence of distant metastatic disease.
