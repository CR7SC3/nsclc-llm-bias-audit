**HPI:**

The patient's initial workup included a chest CT scan which revealed a primary mass in the right upper lobe measuring approximately 4 cm, with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also identified a solitary 1.5 cm nodule in the contralateral (left) lung, suspicious for metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging, which demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, several mediastinal lymph nodes, and the contralateral left lung nodule, consistent with metastatic disease. A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was obtained and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. The pathology from this procedure confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma.
