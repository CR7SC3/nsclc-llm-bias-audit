**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest confirmed the presence of a 4.1 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also revealed prominent right-sided mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy, raising suspicion for locoregional involvement.

For comprehensive staging, a PET/CT was performed. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in ipsilateral mediastinal (paratracheal) and subcarinal lymph nodes. No FDG-avid lesions suggestive of distant metastatic disease were identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or skeleton. A brain MRI with and without contrast was subsequently obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis and nodal staging. Transbronchial needle aspiration (TBNA) was performed on the primary mass and an enlarged subcarinal lymph node. Pathology from both sites confirmed invasive, moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung primary.

These findings are consistent with a locally advanced, non-metastatic process.
