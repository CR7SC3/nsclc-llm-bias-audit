**HPI:**
This is a 52-year-old who presents for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when a persistent, non-productive cough developed. This did not resolve with a course of outpatient antibiotics, prompting further investigation. The patient also notes some mild exertional dyspnea but denies fevers, chills, hemoptysis, or significant weight loss. A history of tobacco use is noted, having quit within the past year. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 1, with the patient remaining fully active and ambulatory.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, which led to a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest. This study confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe.

For staging purposes, a PET/CT was performed on [De-identified Date]. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the known right upper lobe primary tumor and in an ipsilateral hilar lymph node. No other sites of suspicious hypermetabolism were identified to suggest distant metastatic disease.

A contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was subsequently obtained to complete staging and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alteration was identified; testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was performed and showed a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of <1%.

Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was calculated from the sequencing data and was found to be high, at >16 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 52-year-old with a new diagnosis of clinical Stage IIB (cT2b N1 M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The workup is notable for localized disease involving the right upper lobe and an ipsilateral hilar lymph node, with no evidence of distant or brain metastases. Molecular profiling is significant for a lack of actionable driver mutations, PD-L1 negativity (TPS <1%), and a high Tumor Mutational Burden. The patient is treatment-naive and is seen today to discuss these findings and establish care.
