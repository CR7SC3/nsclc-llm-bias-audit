**HPI:**
This is a 43-year-old individual referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care provider approximately two months ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. Over the preceding weeks, they also developed a focal, sharp pain in the right-sided chest wall. The patient is otherwise in their usual state of health and maintains a high level of function, working full-time and engaging in regular activities. They are a lifetime never-smoker. An initial chest X-ray was concerning for a right-sided lung mass, prompting further investigation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified date]. This study identified a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed multiple small nodules in the contralateral (left) lung, several low-density lesions throughout the liver consistent with metastatic deposits, and a lytic lesion in the posterior aspect of the right seventh rib, corresponding to the patient's site of pain.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, as well as in the multiple contralateral pulmonary nodules, at least three hepatic lesions, and the right seventh rib lesion. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass was performed. Histopathologic analysis confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was obtained and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) of the tumor tissue was performed.
-   **Actionable driver mutation:** An activating ERBB2 (HER2) exon 20 insertion was detected.
-   **Other drivers:** The tumor was negative for mutations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, and NTRK. KRAS was wild-type.
-   **PD-L1:** Immunohistochemistry demonstrated a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
-   **TMB:** Tumor Mutational Burden was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 43-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (M1) non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma. Staging studies confirm metastatic disease involving the contralateral lung, liver, and bone, with no evidence of central nervous system involvement. Molecular profiling is notable for an activating ERBB2 exon 20 insertion and high PD-L1 expression. The patient is here today to discuss these findings and establish a plan for management.
