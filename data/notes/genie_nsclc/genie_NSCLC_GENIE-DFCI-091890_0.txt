**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which identified a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal adenopathy. The scan also revealed a solitary, suspicious 2 cm lesion in the right lobe of the liver.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was obtained for staging purposes. This confirmed high avidity in the primary right upper lobe lung mass and mediastinal lymph nodes. Additionally, it demonstrated a hypermetabolic focus in the liver, consistent with a distant metastasis.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed by Interventional Radiology. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, with features consistent with a lung primary.

To complete staging, a brain MRI was performed, which was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastasis.

Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer.
