**HPI:**
This is a 60-year-old individual referred for initial consultation and management of a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a current smoker and presented to their primary care physician approximately two months ago with a persistent dry cough and a new sensation of mild shortness of breath with significant exertion. They deny hemoptysis, chest pain, or unintentional weight loss. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right-sided lung opacity, prompting further diagnostic evaluation. The patient has no significant past medical history and remains fully active and independent with an ECOG performance status of 0. They are here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A comprehensive diagnostic workup was initiated following the abnormal chest radiograph.

- A CT of the chest with contrast was performed, which identified a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the apical segment of the right upper lobe. There was also evidence of ipsilateral hilar adenopathy. The contralateral lung, pleura, and visualized upper abdomen were unremarkable.

- Subsequently, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, moderately differentiated.

- For purposes of staging, a whole-body PET/CT was performed. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the primary right upper lobe mass and in ipsilateral hilar lymph nodes. Critically, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the mediastinum, contralateral lung, or at any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease.

- A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was completed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the biopsy specimen. The key findings are as follows:
- Actionable driver mutation detected: KRAS G12C
- Negative for pathogenic mutations in: EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, ERBB2
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was positive (specific TPS percentage not available).
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 60-year-old with a new diagnosis of non-metastatic, Stage IIB (pT3N0M0, AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology.
