**Diagnostic Workup:**
Initial imaging with a CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis confirmed a primary mass in the right upper lobe of the lung, measuring approximately 4.5 cm. The scan also identified several enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes, highly suspicious for regional involvement. There was no evidence of distant metastatic disease in the abdomen or pelvis. A subsequent PET/CT scan confirmed avid metabolic activity in the right upper lobe mass and in multiple mediastinal lymph node stations, consistent with locoregional disease. No other sites of abnormal metabolic activity suggestive of distant metastases were identified. Biopsy of the right upper lobe mass, performed via bronchoscopy, confirmed adenocarcinoma histology. Further staging procedures, including an endobronchial ultrasound-guided transbronchial needle aspiration (EBUS-TBNA) of mediastinal lymph nodes, confirmed metastatic adenocarcinoma in these nodes. A dedicated MRI of the brain was performed, which showed no evidence of intracranial metastatic disease. Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer.
