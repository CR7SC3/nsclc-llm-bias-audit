**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old who presents for initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker and was in an excellent state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when a persistent, non-productive cough developed. The cough did not resolve with a course of antibiotics. This prompted a chest X-ray which revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity. The patient denies any associated hemoptysis, chest pain, fevers, or unintentional weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0, with the patient maintaining all usual activities of daily living without restriction.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified date]. This confirmed the presence of a 3.8 cm solid mass in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT scan was performed from the skull base to the mid-thighs. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the known right upper lobe mass. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in any regional lymph nodes or at any distant sites. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also obtained as part of the standard staging evaluation and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass was subsequently performed on [De-identified date]. The pathology from this procedure confirmed the diagnosis.

**Molecular Studies:**
Pathology from the right upper lobe mass biopsy confirmed non-small cell lung cancer with adenocarcinoma histology. Comprehensive next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor tissue.
- **Actionable Driver Mutations:** An EGFR exon 20 insertion was detected.
- **Other Markers:** Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and KRAS.
- **Immunotherapy Markers:**
    - PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was strongly positive with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
    - Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 55-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the presence of an EGFR exon 20 insertion and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The case was discussed, and the findings and their implications were reviewed in detail with the patient.
