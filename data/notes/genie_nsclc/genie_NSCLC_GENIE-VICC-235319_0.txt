**History of Present Illness:**
The patient is a 62-year-old with a history of former tobacco use who initially presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. This was associated with a 10-pound unintentional weight loss over the preceding two months. An initial chest radiograph was concerning for a right-sided pulmonary mass, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise active with an ECOG performance status of 1, remaining fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which identified a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also revealed extensive mediastinal and supraclavicular lymphadenopathy, including bulky subcarinal and contralateral (left) paratracheal nodes, as well as an enlarged left supraclavicular node. No suspicious hepatic, adrenal, or osseous lesions were identified.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging purposes. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and confirmed hypermetabolism in the extensive mediastinal and left supraclavicular nodal stations, consistent with nodal metastases. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the body to suggest distant metastatic involvement.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis. Fine-needle aspiration of a subcarinal lymph node (station 7) was performed. Pathologic analysis of the specimen confirmed poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Based on the combination of a primary tumor with contralateral mediastinal and supraclavicular nodal involvement (N3) and no evidence of distant metastases (M0), the patient is diagnosed with AJCC 8th Edition clinical Stage IIIB non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
- **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1-49% range.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was determined to be high (>16 mutations/megabase).
