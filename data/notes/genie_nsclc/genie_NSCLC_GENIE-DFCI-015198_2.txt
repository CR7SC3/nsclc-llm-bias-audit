**HPI:**
This is a 73-year-old individual with a history of former tobacco use who is seen in our oncology clinic for an initial consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough. This did not resolve with a course of antibiotics from their primary care provider, prompting a chest X-ray which revealed a right-sided pulmonary nodule.

The patient reports a good energy level and maintains an excellent performance status (ECOG 0-1), continuing with all activities of daily living without limitation. There has been no significant weight loss, fevers, night sweats, or hemoptysis. Appetite is reported as good. The patient denies any new headaches, focal weakness, or other neurologic symptoms.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a diagnostic CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study confirmed the presence of a 3.8 cm spiculated solid mass in the right upper lobe. There was no associated mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

For purposes of staging, a PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG avidity within the known right upper lobe mass. Importantly, there was no evidence of hypermetabolic activity in any regional lymph node stations or at any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (NOS).

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor specimen. The results are as follows:
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1–49% range.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).
- A loss-of-function mutation was detected in the STK11 gene.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 73-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IB (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, NOS histology, of the right upper lobe. The patient is treatment-naive and has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling is notable for PD-L1 expression of 1-49% and an STK11 loss-of-function mutation, with no other identified actionable driver alterations.
