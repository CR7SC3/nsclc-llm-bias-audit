**HPI:**
This is a 68-year-old individual with a history of current smoking who is referred for initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to an outside provider several weeks ago with a persistent, dry cough. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious pulmonary nodule, which prompted further investigation. A subsequent contrast-enhanced CT of the chest confirmed a solid, spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The patient has not experienced hemoptysis, significant dyspnea, chest pain, or bone pain. Appetite has been stable with no unintentional weight loss. The patient remains fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 0-1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT scan of the chest identified a 2.7 cm solid mass in the right upper lobe. No mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was appreciated.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass on [De-identified Date]. Histopathologic analysis confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.

Staging evaluation was completed. A PET/CT scan demonstrated FDG-avidity confined to the primary right upper lobe mass, without evidence of hypermetabolic activity in regional lymph nodes or any sites of distant disease. A brain MRI with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IB (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing performed on the tumor tissue revealed no actionable driver alterations. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of 1–49%.

Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 68-year-old current smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IB (AJCC 8th Edition) pulmonary adenocarcinoma of the right upper lobe. Comprehensive staging workup is negative for regional nodal involvement or distant metastatic disease. Molecular profiling is notable for the absence of targetable driver mutations and a PD-L1 TPS of 1-49%. The patient has an excellent performance status and is seen today for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
