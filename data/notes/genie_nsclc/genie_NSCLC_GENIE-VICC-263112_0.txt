**HPI:**
This is a 58-year-old individual with a significant smoking history who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation when they developed a persistent, dry cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. This was initially managed with outpatient antibiotics without improvement. Due to worsening symptoms, a chest radiograph was obtained which showed a right-sided lung mass, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise active with a good performance status, reporting no significant weight loss, fevers, or hemoptysis.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study confirmed a spiculated 4.5 cm mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed a suspicious 1.8 cm nodule in the left lower lobe, concerning for metastatic disease. No other sites of thoracic or abdominopelvic metastatic disease were identified.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and the contralateral left lower lobe nodule. There was also FDG uptake in mediastinal lymph nodes. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were seen in the neck, abdomen, pelvis, or skeleton.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was completed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic analysis confirmed the diagnosis of poorly differentiated squamous cell carcinoma.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor biopsy specimen.
- Histology: Squamous cell carcinoma.
- PD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score (TPS): 35% (in the 1-49% range).
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB): Low (<2 mutations/Megabase).
- Molecular profiling did not identify any actionable driver alterations. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 58-year-old current smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV squamous non-small cell lung cancer, metastatic to the contralateral lung. Staging workup is notable for a primary right upper lobe mass with a single site of distant metastasis in the left lung, and no evidence of brain metastases. The patient has a good performance status, estimated at ECOG 1. Molecular studies show a PD-L1 TPS of 1-49% and low TMB, with no actionable driver mutations identified. The patient is here today for an initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and the goals of care.
