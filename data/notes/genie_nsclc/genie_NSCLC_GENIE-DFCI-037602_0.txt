**HPI:**
This is a 61-year-old with a recent diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer who is seen today for initial oncology consultation. The patient is a former smoker, having quit within the last year. The patient initially presented to their primary care provider several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea. An initial chest X-ray was concerning for a right-sided lung mass, prompting further investigation. The patient has maintained a good performance status (ECOG 1) and has no significant constitutional symptoms such as fevers or weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study identified a 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed a suspicious 1.2 cm nodule in the contralateral left lower lobe. No other suspicious thoracic or abdominopelvic metastatic disease was identified.

A PET/CT was subsequently performed for staging. This confirmed high FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the contralateral left lower lobe nodule, consistent with metastatic disease. No other sites of FDG-avid disease were seen in the chest, abdomen, or pelvis.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the primary right upper lobe mass was performed on [De-identified Date]. Pathology returned as adenocarcinoma of the lung.

Given the findings, a brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained to complete staging, which was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue from the right upper lobe biopsy.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified.
- Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 61-year-old former smoker who presents for initial consultation for a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastatic disease to the contralateral lung. Molecular profiling is notable for TMB-intermediate status and no identified actionable driver alterations. The patient is treatment-naive and is here to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
