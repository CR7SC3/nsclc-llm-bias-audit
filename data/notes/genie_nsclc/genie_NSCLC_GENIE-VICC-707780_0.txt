**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a staging CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with intravenous contrast was performed. This study confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe with associated mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. Given the new onset of headaches, a brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained. This study demonstrated multiple small, ring-enhancing lesions in the bilateral cerebral hemispheres, consistent with metastatic disease.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was subsequently performed. Pathologic review of the tissue specimen confirmed a diagnosis of poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alterations were identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.
