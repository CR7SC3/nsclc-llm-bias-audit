**HPI:**
This is a 76-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit over one year ago) who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider, who ordered a chest radiograph which revealed a right upper lobe pulmonary nodule. The patient denies any associated hemoptysis, chest pain, fevers, or unintentional weight loss. They remain fully active and ambulatory, with an excellent performance status (ECOG 0).

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic and staging workup was initiated.

A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest confirmed a 2.8 cm spiculated solid mass in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

A subsequent PET/CT was performed for staging purposes. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe mass (SUVmax 9.8). No other sites of abnormal FDG uptake were identified in the chest or elsewhere in the body to suggest nodal or distant metastatic disease.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained for central nervous system staging and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastasis.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass on [De-identified]. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of poorly differentiated squamous cell carcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung cancer primary.

Based on the above workup, the patient's disease is formally staged as AJCC 8th Edition Stage IB (pT2a N0 M0).

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
-   **Actionable driver(s):** A MET exon 14 skipping mutation was detected.
-   **Negative for:** EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2 alterations.
-   **PD-L1:** Immunohistochemistry (IHC) testing for PD-L1 shows positive expression with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1-49% range.
-   **TMB:** Tumor Mutational Burden is low (<2 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 76-year-old former smoker who presents for initial consultation for a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, Stage IB (T2aN0M0) squamous non-small cell lung cancer of the right lung. The workup has been negative for metastatic disease. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the presence of a MET exon 14 skipping mutation and PD-L1 expression of 1-49%. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and potential management pathways.
