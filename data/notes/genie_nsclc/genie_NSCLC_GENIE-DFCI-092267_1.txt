**HPI:**
This is a 69-year-old individual with a history of former tobacco use (quit >1 year ago) who is referred for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. These symptoms prompted an evaluation with their primary care provider, which initiated a diagnostic workup. The patient reports some fatigue but is fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, with an estimated ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray was concerning for a right-sided pulmonary mass. This was followed by a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis on [De-identified Date]. Imaging revealed a large primary mass in the right lower lobe of the lung with associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy. A solitary, enhancing mass was also noted in the left adrenal gland, suspicious for a distant metastasis.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the primary lung mass. Pathology from this specimen returned as non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (NOS).

For definitive staging, a PET/CT scan was performed. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right lower lobe mass, multiple mediastinal lymph nodes, and the left adrenal mass. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was negative for intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue with the following results:
*   **Actionable Driver Mutation:** KRAS G12C mutation detected.
*   **Other Drivers:** The tumor was negative for alterations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and ERBB2.
*   **Immunotherapy Markers:**
    *   PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry was positive (specific tumor proportion score percentage is pending).
    *   Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 69-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, NOS histology, with a solitary metastasis to the adrenal gland. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for a KRAS G12C mutation, positive PD-L1 expression, and an intermediate tumor mutational burden. The patient is treatment-naive.
