**HPI:**
This is an initial consultation for an 80-year-old with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over a year ago. The patient has a past medical history notable for a prior melanoma, which has been managed and is not active. The patient initially presented to an outside facility several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest radiograph was obtained which was suspicious for a right-sided lung mass, prompting further evaluation. Subsequently, the patient developed new-onset, progressive lower back pain, which has been managed with over-the-counter analgesics with limited relief. The patient is here today for an initial oncology evaluation and to discuss the diagnosis.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe, associated with mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The scan also revealed a lytic lesion in the L3 vertebral body, suspicious for metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging, which demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and the mediastinal nodes. Additionally, there was a corresponding hypermetabolic focus in the L3 vertebral body, confirming the suspicion of a solitary site of osseous metastasis.

A CT-guided core biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed. Pathology confirmed poorly differentiated non-small cell carcinoma with features most consistent with adenocarcinoma.

Given the diagnosis of Stage IV disease, a brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for staging, which was negative for intracranial metastases.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the lung biopsy was sent for next-generation sequencing (NGS) and ancillary studies.
-   **Driver Mutations:** An actionable KRAS G12C mutation was detected. No pathogenic mutations were identified in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, or ERBB2.
-   **Immunotherapy Markers:** PD-L1 expression by tumor proportion score (TPS) is positive at 1-49%. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) is intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
-   **Resistance Markers:** Sequencing also identified a loss-of-function mutation in STK11.

**Problem Summary:**
This is an 80-year-old, treatment-naive patient with a new diagnosis of AJCC Stage IV (cT3 N2 M1b) non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology, with a solitary site of metastasis to the bone. Molecular testing is notable for a KRAS G12C mutation, PD-L1 expression of 1-49%, and a concurrent STK11 loss-of-function mutation. The patient is seen today for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
