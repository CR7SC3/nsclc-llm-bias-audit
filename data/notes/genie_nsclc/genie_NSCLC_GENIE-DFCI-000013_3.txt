**HPI:**
This is an initial consultation for an 80-year-old patient presenting for evaluation of newly diagnosed non-small cell lung cancer. The patient first noted a persistent cough and increasing fatigue approximately three months ago. Over the past several weeks, they also experienced some unintentional weight loss, estimated to be around 10 pounds. They initially attributed these symptoms to aging and seasonal allergies but sought medical attention from their primary care physician when the cough worsened and became more productive. The patient reports an ECOG performance status of 1, able to carry out light work and activities of daily living without significant difficulty, but experiencing some limitation with more strenuous activities.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Initial evaluation by the primary care physician included a chest X-ray, which revealed a suspicious right upper lobe mass. This prompted a CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis, which confirmed a primary lung mass measuring approximately 4.5 cm in the right upper lobe. The CT also identified enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes and a new, suspicious lesion in the left adrenal gland, highly concerning for metastatic disease. A subsequent PET/CT scan demonstrated avid uptake in the right upper lobe mass, the mediastinal lymph nodes, and the left adrenal lesion, consistent with malignancy. An MRI of the brain was performed to complete staging and showed no evidence of intracranial metastatic disease. A CT-guided biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed, and pathology confirmed non-small cell lung carcinoma, adenocarcinoma histology. Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Molecular profiling was performed on the tumor biopsy specimen. Results indicate no actionable driver alteration identified, specifically negative for EGFR mutations, ALK rearrangements, ROS1 rearrangements, BRAF V600E mutations, MET exon 14 skipping alterations, RET fusions, and NTRK fusions. PD-L1 expression status is not yet available.

**Problem Summary:**
This is an 80-year-old patient with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastatic disease to the left adrenal gland. There is no evidence of brain metastases. Molecular testing revealed no actionable driver alterations. The patient presents for initial oncology evaluation and discussion of further management.
