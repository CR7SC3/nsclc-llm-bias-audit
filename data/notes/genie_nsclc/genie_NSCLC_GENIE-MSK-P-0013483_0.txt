**HPI:**
This is the initial medical oncology consultation for a 58-year-old individual referred for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker who was in their usual state of health until approximately six weeks prior to this evaluation, when they developed a persistent, dry cough. This did not resolve with a course of conservative management. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained which demonstrated a right-sided pulmonary opacity. The patient was subsequently referred for further diagnostic workup. The patient denies fevers, chills, night sweats, or significant unintentional weight loss. They remain fully ambulatory and active, with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed the presence of a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. The scan also revealed prominent right-sided mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy, suspicious for nodal involvement. No distant visceral, adrenal, or osseous metastases were identified.

For definitive staging, a whole-body PET/CT scan was performed. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the known right upper lobe primary mass. Avid uptake was also noted in ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes (station 4R), consistent with nodal metastases. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease below the diaphragm or in the contralateral hemithorax.

To complete staging and rule out central nervous system involvement, a brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained, which was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent flexible bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue acquisition. Fine-needle aspiration (FNA) was performed on the primary right upper lobe mass and the enlarged station 4R lymph node. Cytopathology from both sites was consistent with invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Initial biomarker analysis has been performed on the tissue specimen.
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry is negative (Tumor Proportion Score <1%).
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) is low (<2 mut/Mb).
- A comprehensive next-generation sequencing panel to assess for targetable genomic alterations has been sent, and these results are currently pending.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 58-year-old never-smoker who presents for evaluation of a new cancer diagnosis. The comprehensive diagnostic workup, including imaging and tissue sampling, has established a diagnosis of Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with no evidence of distant metastatic disease. Initial molecular testing is notable for PD-L1 negative and TMB-low status, with further genomic results pending.
