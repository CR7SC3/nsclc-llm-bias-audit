**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old individual with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, seen today for initial oncology consultation. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker and was in their usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to diagnosis, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially treated with a course of antibiotics without improvement. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained which showed a right-sided lung opacity, prompting further investigation with a CT scan. The patient reports good energy levels and maintains a high level of activity without significant shortness of breath. They deny fevers, chills, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed on [De-identified Date]. This revealed a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also demonstrated a suspicious 1.5 cm nodule in the contralateral left lower lobe, concerning for metastatic disease. No other sites of distant disease were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones.

A subsequent PET/CT scan confirmed FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in the contralateral left lung nodule, consistent with metastatic disease. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for staging and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastasis.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathology confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung malignancy.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the lung biopsy was sent for comprehensive genomic profiling via next-generation sequencing.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was negative, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of <1%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 55-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastasis to the contralateral lung. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular testing is notable for the absence of an actionable driver mutation and negative PD-L1 expression.
