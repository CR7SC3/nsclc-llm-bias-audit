**HPI:**
This is a 66-year-old individual with a significant history of tobacco use who is referred for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to primary care approximately two months ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. An initial chest radiograph revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient is otherwise active and maintains a good functional status, reporting the ability to perform all activities of daily living without assistance, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 1. The patient is a current smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed for staging purposes. Imaging confirmed a 4.5 cm primary mass in the right upper lobe. Additionally, several small nodules were identified in the contralateral left lung, the largest measuring 1.8 cm, highly suspicious for metastatic disease. There was no evidence of metastatic involvement in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A subsequent MRI of the brain was performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the primary right upper lobe mass on [De-identified date]. Histopathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
The biopsy specimen was submitted for comprehensive molecular profiling and immunohistochemistry.
- **PD-L1:** Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) is positive in the 1–49% range.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Results indicate a high TMB at >16 mut/Mb.
- **Next-Generation Sequencing:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was found to be negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, KRAS, and ERBB2.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 66-year-old individual with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastasis to the contralateral lung. The patient is treatment-naive with an excellent performance status (ECOG 1). Molecular studies are notable for a PD-L1 TPS of 1-49% and high TMB, with no targetable driver mutation identified.
