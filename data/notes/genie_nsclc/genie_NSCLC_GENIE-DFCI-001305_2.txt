**History of Present Illness:**

Initial workup included a chest X-ray which revealed a right upper lobe opacity. This prompted a contrast-enhanced CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis for further characterization. **Diagnostic Workup:**
Staging investigations have been completed. A diagnostic CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis demonstrated a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the posterior aspect of the right upper lobe. This confirmed intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right paratracheal nodal station, consistent with the CT findings. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere to suggest distant metastases. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.
