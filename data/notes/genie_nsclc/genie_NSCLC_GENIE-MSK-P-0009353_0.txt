**HPI:**
This is a 37-year-old individual with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, presenting for initial oncology consultation. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker. The patient was in a usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when a persistent, non-productive cough developed. Over the subsequent weeks, the patient also noted the onset of mild, constant right upper quadrant abdominal discomfort and a sense of fatigue. This prompted an evaluation with a primary care provider, who ordered initial imaging. The patient has a good performance status, fully ambulatory and capable of all self-care, and continues to work.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with intravenous contrast was performed. Imaging revealed a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the upper lobe of the right lung, associated with mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Multiple hypodense lesions were identified throughout the liver, the largest measuring up to 4.1 cm, highly suspicious for metastatic deposits. A 2.5 cm nodule was also noted in the left adrenal gland, also concerning for a metastasis.

Given the imaging findings, the patient underwent an ultrasound-guided core biopsy of a representative hepatic lesion. Pathology from this procedure confirmed metastatic adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemical staining was positive for TTF-1 and Napsin-A, consistent with a lung primary origin.

Staging was completed with a brain MRI with and without contrast, which showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

Based on this workup, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the liver biopsy tissue specimen. Key findings are as follows:
*   **Actionable Driver:** An ALK gene rearrangement was detected.
*   **Other Markers:** The tumor was negative for actionable mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   **PD-L1:** Per the sequencing panel utilized (pre-2017 standard), PD-L1 expression was not tested.
*   **TMB:** Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 37-year-old never-smoker presenting with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The disease is characterized by an ALK gene rearrangement. Staging studies confirm metastatic disease involving the liver and left adrenal gland, with no evidence of central nervous system involvement. The patient is treatment-naive.
