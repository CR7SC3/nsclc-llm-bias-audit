**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 51-year-old individual with a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, referred for initial consultation and discussion of management. The patient has a history as a former smoker, having quit within the past year.

The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation when they developed a persistent, dry cough. This did not resolve with conservative measures, prompting a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious opacity in the right lung, which led to a more comprehensive diagnostic workup.

The patient denies constitutional symptoms such as fevers, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. They report no hemoptysis, chest pain, or dyspnea at rest. Energy levels are good, and the patient remains fully active and engaged in all usual activities. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed. This confirmed the presence of a 2.2 cm spiculated solid nodule in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy. The remainder of the visualized lung parenchyma, pleura, and upper abdomen were unremarkable.

For definitive staging, a whole-body PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity localized to the known right upper lobe nodule. No other sites of abnormal hypermetabolism were identified to suggest nodal or distant metastatic disease. A contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule. Pathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, primary to the lung.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor tissue.
-   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **PD-L1 Expression:** PD-L1 expression was strongly positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was low, reported as <2 mutations/megabase.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 51-year-old with an excellent performance status and a new diagnosis of clinical Stage IA (cT1bN0M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup including PET/CT and brain MRI is negative for regional or distant metastatic disease. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%) but is negative for any identified actionable oncogenic driver mutations. The patient is treatment-naive and presents today to discuss the diagnosis and management options.
