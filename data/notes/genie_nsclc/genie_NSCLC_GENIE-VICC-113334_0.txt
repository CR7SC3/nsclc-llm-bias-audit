**HPI:**
This is a 70-year-old with a significant smoking history who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to diagnosis, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea. When symptoms did not resolve, they presented to their primary care provider. An initial chest x-ray demonstrated a right upper lobe opacity, prompting further investigation. The patient denies fevers, chills, or significant hemoptysis but does report an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds over the past three months. They remain fully ambulatory and independent in all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified Date], which confirmed a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. There was associated ipsilateral hilar and intrapulmonary lymphadenopathy noted, with the largest node measuring 1.5 cm. No suspicious hepatic, adrenal, or osseous lesions were identified. The visualized portions of the abdomen and pelvis were negative for metastatic disease.

For definitive tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass on [De-identified Date]. Pathology confirmed poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, with immunohistochemistry consistent with a lung primary.

Subsequent staging evaluation included a PET/CT scan, which demonstrated intense FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and the previously noted ipsilateral hilar and intrapulmonary lymph nodes. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease elsewhere in the chest or in the abdomen or pelvis to suggest distant metastases. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastatic disease.

This comprehensive workup is consistent with a diagnosis of AJCC 8th Edition Stage IIB non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was specifically negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was not tested. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 70-year-old current smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IIB non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma of the right lung. The workup has been completed and confirms localized disease without evidence of distant metastases. Molecular profiling did not identify any targetable driver alterations. The patient presents today to discuss the diagnosis in detail and to review the goals of care and potential management strategies.
