**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old with a history of being a never-smoker, who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea. Initial treatment for a presumed bronchitis was unsuccessful. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest X-ray was obtained, which revealed a right upper lobe opacity. The patient was subsequently referred to a pulmonologist for further evaluation. The patient reports a 10-pound unintentional weight loss over the past three months but otherwise denies fevers, night sweats, or hemoptysis. Performance status is excellent, ECOG 1, with the patient continuing to work and perform all activities of daily living with minimal limitation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A CT of the chest with contrast was performed on [De-identified], which identified a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. The mass was noted to abut the major fissure. Additionally, there was significant ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy, with the largest lymph node measuring 3.1 cm in short axis.

A subsequent PET/CT scan on [De-identified] demonstrated intense FDG avidity in the right upper lobe primary mass (SUVmax 12.4) and in the right paratracheal, precarinal, and subcarinal lymph nodes (SUVmax 9.8), consistent with the CT findings. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease below the diaphragm or in the contralateral hemithorax, and no findings suggestive of distant metastatic disease.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for staging purposes and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastasis.

On [De-identified], the patient underwent an endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) with fine-needle aspiration (FNA) of a subcarinal (station 7) lymph node. Pathology from the EBUS-FNA confirmed metastatic adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the EBUS-FNA. No actionable driver alteration was identified. Specifically, the tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was not tested. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was reported as intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 55-year-old never-smoker who presents with a new diagnosis of Stage IIIA (cT2bN2M0, AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The diagnosis was established via EBUS-FNA of a mediastinal lymph node. Staging workup, including PET/CT and brain MRI, is negative for distant metastatic disease. Comprehensive molecular profiling of the tumor did not identify any actionable driver mutations. The patient is treatment-naive and is seen today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
