Past medical history is notable for a remote history of colon cancer, for which the patient was treated and remains under routine surveillance without evidence of recurrence. **DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
Following the abnormal chest x-ray, a diagnostic CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with intravenous contrast was performed. This study characterized a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. Additionally, the scan demonstrated prominent ipsilateral mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy, concerning for regional metastatic involvement. No suspicious lesions were identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones.

For definitive staging, a whole-body PET/CT was subsequently performed. This confirmed intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in multiple ipsilateral mediastinal lymph node stations (subcarinal and right paratracheal), consistent with nodal metastases. No distant FDG-avid metastatic disease was identified.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient ultimately underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathologic analysis confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.
