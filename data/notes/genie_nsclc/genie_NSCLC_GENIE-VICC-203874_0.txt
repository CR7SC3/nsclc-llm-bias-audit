The patient initially presented to their primary care provider several months ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious nodule in the right lung, prompting further investigation. The patient reports feeling generally well otherwise, with no significant weight loss, fevers, or hemoptysis. Performance status is excellent; patient is fully active and without restriction (ECOG 0).
