**HPI:**
This 36-year-old is seen today in the Thoracic Oncology clinic for an initial consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over one year ago. They were in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive right-sided rib cage pain. The pain was initially managed with over-the-counter analgesics with minimal relief. Due to the persistence of symptoms, they presented to their primary care provider for evaluation. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe lung mass. The patient was subsequently referred for further oncologic workup. They report mild fatigue but are otherwise fully ambulatory and managing all activities of daily living. ECOG performance status is 1.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified]. This study confirmed a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. It also revealed multiple enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes and a lytic lesion in the right 7th rib, concerning for metastatic disease.

A PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained for staging purposes. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe primary mass, as well as in mediastinal lymph nodes and the previously identified lytic lesion in the right 7th rib. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified in the neck, abdomen, or pelvis.

An MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the lung biopsy was sent for next-generation sequencing and immunohistochemistry. The results are as follows:
*   **Histology:** Adenocarcinoma
*   **Actionable Driver Mutation:** RET fusion detected.
*   **Other Drivers:** Negative for pathogenic mutations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   **PD-L1 Expression:** Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) ≥50%.
*   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 36-year-old who presents with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Staging studies confirm a primary lung mass with metastatic disease to bone. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the presence of a RET fusion and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The patient is here today to discuss the implications of this diagnosis.
