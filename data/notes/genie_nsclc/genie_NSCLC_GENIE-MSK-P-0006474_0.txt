**HPI:**
This is a 64-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit over one year ago) who is seen today for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to primary care approximately six weeks ago with a persistent dry cough and mild, progressive dyspnea on exertion. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, which prompted further investigation. The patient denies any hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or significant weight loss. The patient remains fully ambulatory and independent in all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This study characterized a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. It also demonstrated suspicious ipsilateral hilar and peribronchial lymphadenopathy.

For definitive staging, a whole-body PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained. This study showed intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe primary mass and in the right hilar lymph nodes. Critically, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the contralateral lung, mediastinum, or at any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease. A brain MRI with and without contrast was also performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue acquisition. A transbronchial needle aspiration of a right hilar lymph node was performed. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, with adenocarcinoma histology.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor tissue. The key findings are as follows:
- Actionable driver mutation detected: KRAS G12C.
- The panel was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by immunohistochemistry showed a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 64-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic Stage IIB non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. The tumor is characterized by a KRAS G12C mutation and high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%). The patient has an excellent performance status and is here to be evaluated for management options.
