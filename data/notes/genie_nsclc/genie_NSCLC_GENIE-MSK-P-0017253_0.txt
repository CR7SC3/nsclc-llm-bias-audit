A subsequent CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. To complete staging, a PET/CT scan demonstrated hypermetabolic activity in the primary lung lesion, mediastinal lymph nodes, and the hepatic lesions. An MRI of the brain was also conducted, which showed no evidence of intracranial metastatic disease. Based on the comprehensive staging workup, the patient's cancer is designated as AJCC Stage IV.

**Molecular Studies:**
Molecular profiling of the tumor tissue was performed. Results indicated no actionable driver alterations, specifically negative for EGFR mutations, ALK rearrangements, ROS1 rearrangements, BRAF mutations, MET exon 14 skipping alterations, RET fusions, and NTRK fusions.
