**HPI:**
This is a 49-year-old who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and mild exertional dyspnea. An initial course of antibiotics from their primary care physician provided no relief. Due to worsening symptoms and the patient's history as a former smoker (quit within the last year), a chest X-ray was obtained, which revealed a right-sided lung mass. The patient has remained active with a good performance status (ECOG 1), able to perform all activities of daily living with minimal shortness of breath. They report an unintentional weight loss of approximately 5 pounds over the last two months. They deny hemoptysis, fevers, or significant chest pain. The patient is here today to review the completed diagnostic workup and discuss the diagnosis.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A staging CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed following the abnormal chest X-ray. This imaging confirmed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. In addition, a 1.5 cm nodule was identified in the contralateral left lower lobe, suspicious for metastatic disease. There was evidence of mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy. No other sites of distant metastatic disease were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones on the portions of the skeleton imaged.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was performed and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was subsequently performed. Pathology confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (NOS).

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue.
- **Actionable driver(s) detected:** MET amplification (high-level).
- **Testing was negative for alterations in:** EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 expression:** Negative (Tumor Proportion Score 0%).
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 49-year-old with a new diagnosis of AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer (NOS histology), with metastatic disease to the contralateral lung. The tumor is characterized by high-level MET amplification and is PD-L1 negative. The patient is treatment-naive and presents for initial consultation.
