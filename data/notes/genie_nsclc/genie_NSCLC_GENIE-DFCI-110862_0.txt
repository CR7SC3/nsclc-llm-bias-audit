**HPI:**
This is a 67-year-old individual with a significant smoking history who is seen today for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to primary care approximately one month ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious opacity in the right lung, prompting further investigation. The patient otherwise feels well, denying fevers, chills, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. Energy levels are reported as good, and the patient remains fully active and independent with all activities of daily living, corresponding to an ECOG performance status of 0.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This confirmed a 2.2 cm solid, spiculated nodule in the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal, hilar, or supraclavicular lymphadenopathy. The remainder of the thoracic and abdominal imaging was negative for evidence of distant metastatic disease.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was obtained for staging. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity confined to the known right upper lobe nodule. No other sites of abnormal metabolic activity were identified to suggest nodal involvement or distant metastases. Brain MRI was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient then underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule. Pathology from this procedure established a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer with adenocarcinoma histology.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue. This analysis did not identify any actionable driver alterations; results were negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was performed, revealing high expression with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of ≥50%. Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 67-year-old with an excellent performance status and a new diagnosis of treatment-naive, non-metastatic, Stage IA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype. Molecular profiling is notable for high PD-L1 expression (TPS ≥50%) and the absence of identified actionable driver mutations.
