**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old who presents for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion. This prompted a visit to their primary care provider. An initial chest X-ray was abnormal, leading to a follow-up CT scan of the chest. The patient is a former smoker, having quit more than one year ago. They report some fatigue but maintain a good performance status, continuing with all activities of daily living without assistance.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A staging CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed. This imaging confirmed the presence of a primary mass in the lung parenchyma and also identified a moderate pleural effusion. There was no evidence of distant metastatic disease in the abdomen or pelvis. A dedicated MRI of the brain was subsequently performed as part of the staging workup, which was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the primary lung mass. Pathological analysis confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma. To both evaluate the effusion and complete staging, a thoracentesis was performed. Cytological analysis of the pleural fluid was positive for malignant cells, confirming metastatic involvement of the pleura.

Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with AJCC Stage IV non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue. The results are as follows:
- **Actionable Drivers Detected:** RET fusion, KRAS G12C mutation.
- **Negative for:** EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, NTRK, ERBB2 alterations.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Positive, Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) 1-49%.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2-16 mut/Mb).
- **Other Significant Findings:** A loss-of-function mutation in STK11 was identified.

**Problem Summary:**
1.  Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer, Adenocarcinoma histology.
2.  AJCC Stage IV disease, with metastasis confined to the pleura at the time of diagnosis. No evidence of brain metastases.
3.  Molecular profile is notable for a RET fusion, a KRAS G12C mutation, and an STK11 loss-of-function mutation.
4.  PD-L1 TPS is positive at 1-49%.
5.  The patient is treatment-naive and is seen today for initial consultation to discuss the diagnosis and establish a plan of care.
