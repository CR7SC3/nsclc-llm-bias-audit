**HPI:**
The patient is a 79-year-old individual presenting for an initial oncology consultation regarding a recent diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to their primary care physician approximately three months ago with a persistent cough, increasing dyspnea on exertion, and a reported unintentional weight loss of 10 pounds over the past two months. More recently, the patient also reported new-onset headaches, described as dull and generalized, along with some mild, intermittent right-sided weakness. An initial chest X-ray revealed a suspicious mass in the left lung, prompting further diagnostic evaluation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed, which confirmed a dominant mass in the left upper lobe of the lung, along with hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy. To establish a diagnosis, a bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound-guided biopsy was performed, which confirmed the presence of adenocarcinoma. Subsequent comprehensive staging included a PET-CT scan, which demonstrated hypermetabolic activity in the primary lung mass and regional lymph nodes, as well as in the left adrenal gland. A dedicated MRI of the brain was also performed due to the new neurological symptoms, which revealed multiple enhancing lesions consistent with brain metastases. Based on these findings, the patient's cancer is staged as AJCC Stage IV. The patient's Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status is currently assessed at 1, reflecting some symptomatology but remaining largely ambulatory and capable of self-care.

**Molecular Studies:**
Molecular profiling was performed on the tumor biopsy specimen. Results indicate no actionable driver alteration identified, with testing negative for EGFR mutations, ALK rearrangements, ROS1 rearrangements, BRAF V600E mutations, MET exon 14 skipping mutations, RET fusions, and NTRK fusions. PD-L1 testing results are not yet reported.

**Problem Summary:**
This 79-year-old patient has a new diagnosis of treatment-naive non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, AJCC Stage IV, with documented brain metastases and left adrenal gland metastasis. Molecular testing has not revealed any targetable driver alterations. The patient is now being evaluated for initiation of systemic therapy.
