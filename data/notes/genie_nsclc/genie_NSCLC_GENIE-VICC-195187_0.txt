**HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS**
This is a 65-year-old with a history of tobacco use who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to an outside facility several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive shortness of breath on exertion. A chest X-ray performed at that time was concerning for a right-sided pulmonary mass, prompting a more extensive diagnostic evaluation. The patient has no prior history of malignancy and has received no cancer-directed therapy to date. The patient is a current smoker. Performance status is ECOG 1, with symptoms being managed at present.

**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP**
A contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed on [De-identified]. This imaging confirmed the presence of a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. The CT also revealed prominent ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy, with the largest node measuring 2.5 cm in short axis. No suspicious lesions were identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or other visualized abdominal organs.

To further evaluate the extent of disease, a whole-body PET/CT scan was subsequently obtained. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right-sided mediastinal and subcarinal lymph nodes, consistent with the CT findings. There was no evidence of FDG-avid disease at any distant sites to suggest metastatic involvement.

A brain MRI with and without contrast was performed for central nervous system staging and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The diagnosis was established via an endobronchial ultrasound-guided fine needle aspiration (EBUS-FNA) of a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung primary.

**MOLECULAR STUDIES**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy specimen.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor is negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression was not tested on this specimen.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was reported as high at >16 mutations/megabase.

**PROBLEM SUMMARY**
In summary, this is a 65-year-old, treatment-naive patient with a new diagnosis of locally advanced, non-metastatic, Stage IIIA (cT2bN2M0) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling of the tumor did not identify any currently targetable driver alterations but did show a high tumor mutational burden. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and the implications of these findings.
