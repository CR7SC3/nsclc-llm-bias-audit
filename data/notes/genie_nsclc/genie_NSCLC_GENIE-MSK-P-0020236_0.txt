**HPI:**
This is a 53-year-old individual with a history of current tobacco use who presents for initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient initially presented to primary care approximately one month ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe pulmonary nodule, which prompted further investigation. The patient denies constitutional symptoms, including fevers, chills, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss. They report a good energy level and maintain an ECOG performance status of 0, remaining fully active and able to carry on all pre-disease activities without restriction.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis was performed for staging. This confirmed the presence of a 2.8 cm solid, spiculated nodule in the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. There was no evidence of mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy. The liver, adrenal glands, and visualized osseous structures were unremarkable, with no findings suggestive of distant metastatic disease. A subsequent brain MRI with and without contrast was also obtained and was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe nodule was performed on [De-identified Date]. Pathologic review of the specimen confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma. Based on these findings, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IB disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tissue from the core biopsy was sent for next-generation sequencing. The histology was confirmed as adenocarcinoma. Molecular profiling identified an actionable sensitizing EGFR L858R point mutation. Further analysis was negative for ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2 alterations. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) was intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb). PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 53-year-old current smoker, treatment-naive, who presents for initial consultation for a newly diagnosed Stage IB (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with a sensitizing EGFR L858R mutation.
