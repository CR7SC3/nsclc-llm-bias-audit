**HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:**
This is a 79-year-old individual referred for initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately six weeks ago, when they began to experience progressive shortness of breath with exertion and a persistent dry cough. These symptoms prompted a visit to their primary care physician, which initiated a diagnostic workup.

The patient reports feeling generally well otherwise, with a good appetite and stable weight. They remain fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living, though their exercise tolerance is limited by dyspnea. Performance status is estimated as ECOG 1. Of note, the patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
Initial evaluation began with a chest X-ray that showed a right-sided pleural effusion, leading to a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest. The CT scan confirmed a large right pleural effusion and identified an underlying 3.5 cm spiculated mass in the right lower lobe, as well as diffuse pleural thickening.

For staging, a PET/CT scan was performed. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right lower lobe mass and diffuse, avid uptake throughout the right pleura, consistent with malignant pleural disease (M1a). No other sites of FDG-avid metastatic disease were identified in the neck, contralateral thorax, abdomen, or pelvis. A staging MRI of the brain with contrast was negative for intracranial metastases.

A CT-guided thoracentesis was performed, and fluid cytology was positive for adenocarcinoma. This was followed by a video-assisted thoracoscopic (VATS) pleural biopsy to obtain sufficient tissue for molecular analysis. Pathologic review of the pleural tissue confirmed infiltration by a moderately differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a lung primary.

**MOLECULAR STUDIES:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the pleural biopsy tissue. The results are as follows:
- **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or fusions involving *EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET* exon 14, *RET, NTRK, KRAS,* and *ERBB2*.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) of 1–49%.
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**PROBLEM SUMMARY:**
In summary, this is a 79-year-old, treatment-naive never-smoker with an excellent performance status who presents with a new diagnosis of Stage IV non-small cell lung adenocarcinoma, metastatic to the pleura. Comprehensive molecular profiling is negative for targetable driver mutations, with a PD-L1 TPS of 1-49%. The case was discussed at length with the patient.
