**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed, which confirmed a 3.5 cm spiculated solid mass in the right upper lobe. No significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was noted.

Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid disease in the mediastinal or hilar lymph nodes, nor were any distant metastatic sites of disease identified in the chest, abdomen, or pelvis. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IB (T2aN0M0) non-small cell lung cancer.
