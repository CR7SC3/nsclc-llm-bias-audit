**History of Present Illness:**
This is a 48-year-old individual with a recent history of a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive dyspnea on exertion over the last several months. The patient initially sought evaluation with a primary care provider, which led to imaging studies. There is a history of tobacco use, having quit within the past year. The patient is otherwise active, maintaining a good functional status and working full-time. The patient is here today for initial consultation and to establish care following a new diagnosis of lung cancer.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
The patient's initial workup began with a chest radiograph that demonstrated a large right upper lobe opacity. This prompted a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis. The CT scan confirmed the presence of a large primary mass in the right upper lobe with extensive, bulky ipsilateral mediastinal and hilar lymphadenopathy.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was performed. This study showed intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in multiple right-sided mediastinal and hilar lymph nodes. No evidence of distant metastatic disease in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones was identified on the PET/CT. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent a bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis and staging. A transbronchial needle aspiration (TBNA) was performed on a right paratracheal lymph node. Pathologic review of the biopsy specimen confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung cancer primary.

Based on the combination of imaging and pathology, the patient was diagnosed with AJCC 8th Edition Stage IIIB disease.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tissue biopsy specimen. The key findings are as follows:
*   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alterations were identified. The tumor was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

*   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** TMB was found to be in the intermediate range (2–16 mutations/megabase).
*   **Other Findings:** The sequencing panel did identify a loss-of-function mutation in the STK11 gene.
