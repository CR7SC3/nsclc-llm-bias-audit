**HPI:**
This is a 66-year-old individual with a history of a persistent, non-productive cough for approximately two months, which prompted evaluation by a primary care physician. An initial chest X-ray was abnormal, leading to further investigation. The patient reports mild dyspnea on exertion but is otherwise asymptomatic, denying fever, chills, hemoptysis, or significant weight loss. The patient maintains a good functional status, continuing with daily activities and ambulating without difficulty, corresponding to an ECOG performance status of 1. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker. The patient is now seen in our clinic for initial consultation and to establish care following this new diagnosis.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
A diagnostic CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed following an abnormal chest radiograph. The CT scan identified a 4.1 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also revealed multiple enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes and a distinct 1.5 cm nodule in the contralateral left lower lobe, concerning for metastatic disease.

A subsequent PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass, the mediastinal lymph nodes, and the left lower lobe nodule, consistent with widespread malignant disease. No other distant sites of FDG-avid disease were identified in the chest, abdomen, or pelvis.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was completed and showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified date], the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Histopathologic examination of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung cancer primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry (IHC) were performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
- An actionable sensitizing mutation was identified: **EGFR exon 19 deletion**.
- Testing was negative for alterations in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- PD-L1 expression by IHC was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the **1–49%** range.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be **intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb)**.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 66-year-old never-smoker presenting with a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Based on the diagnostic workup, the patient has AJCC Stage IV disease with a primary right upper lobe mass, mediastinal lymphadenopathy, and a contralateral lung metastasis. Staging brain MRI is negative for metastatic disease. Molecular analysis of the tumor is notable for the presence of a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion.
