**HPI:**
This is an 80-year-old individual referred for initial oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to primary care approximately one month ago with a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially managed with a trial of empiric therapies without resolution, prompting further investigation. The patient denies hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or significant weight loss. There is mild dyspnea on exertion but the patient remains fully ambulatory and independent with all activities of daily living. Performance status is excellent, estimated at ECOG 1. Past medical history is notable for a remote history of tobacco use, having quit more than one year prior to presentation.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest radiograph revealed a right upper lobe opacity, leading to a staging CT scan of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with intravenous contrast. This study characterized a 4.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. It also demonstrated prominent ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy concerning for nodal involvement.

A subsequent whole-body PET/CT scan was performed for staging. This confirmed high FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass and in the right paratracheal and subcarinal lymph nodes, consistent with the findings on CT. Importantly, the PET/CT did not reveal any FDG-avid lesions suggestive of distant metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathologic analysis of the tissue established the diagnosis.

**Molecular Studies:**
Pathology from the lung mass biopsy confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma. Comprehensive next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue with the following key results:
*   **Histology:** Non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype.
*   **Actionable Driver Mutations:** A MET exon 14 skipping mutation was detected.
*   **Other Driver Mutations:** Testing was negative for alterations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   **Immunotherapy Biomarkers:**
    *   PD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) is in the 1-49% range.
    *   Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) is intermediate (2-16 mut/Mb).
    *   A loss-of-function mutation in STK11 was identified.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is an 80-year-old, treatment-naive individual with a new diagnosis of AJCC Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The patient has an excellent performance status. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for a MET exon 14 skipping mutation, PD-L1 expression of 1-49%, and co-occurring STK11 loss. The patient is seen today for initial consultation to review these findings.
