They remain fully ambulatory and independent in all activities of daily living, with an estimated ECOG performance status of 1.

An initial chest radiograph revealed a right upper lobe opacity, which led to a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis on [De-identified Date]. On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) and transbronchial needle aspiration of a subcarinal lymph node. Pathologic analysis of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer, not otherwise specified (nos).
