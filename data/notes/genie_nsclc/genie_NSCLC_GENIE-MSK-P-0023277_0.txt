**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old individual with a history of being a former smoker (quit over one year ago) who presents for an initial oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months prior to evaluation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. When the cough did not resolve with a course of conservative management, a chest X-ray was obtained which revealed a right-sided lung opacity. The patient denies fevers, chills, or significant weight loss but does report some mild dyspnea on exertion. They maintain an excellent performance status, continuing with all activities of daily living without restriction.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed. This demonstrated a spiculated mass in the right upper lobe measuring approximately 4 cm. The scan also revealed enlarged ipsilateral mediastinal lymph nodes, concerning for regional metastasis.

For definitive staging, a PET/CT was subsequently performed. This confirmed high FDG-avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in multiple mediastinal nodal stations, including the subcarinal region. No FDG-avid lesions suggestive of distant metastatic disease were identified in the contralateral lung, liver, adrenal glands, or bones. A staging MRI of the brain with contrast was also obtained and was negative for any intracranial metastases.

The patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS) for tissue diagnosis. Biopsies were successfully obtained from the primary right upper lobe mass and an involved mediastinal lymph node. Pathology from both sites confirmed invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a non-small cell lung cancer primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) and immunohistochemistry were performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alterations were identified; results were negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2. PD-L1 expression was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1–49% range. The Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 55-year-old with a new diagnosis of non-metastatic, Stage IIIA non-small cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology. Molecular profiling is notable for positive PD-L1 expression (TPS 1-49%) and the absence of targetable driver mutations. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.
