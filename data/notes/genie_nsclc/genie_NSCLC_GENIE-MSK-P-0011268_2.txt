**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A chest CT with contrast was performed following the abnormal chest x-ray. This study identified a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. There was associated right hilar and ipsilateral mediastinal lymphadenopathy noted. The visualized portions of the upper abdomen, including the liver and adrenal glands, were unremarkable.

To complete staging, a whole-body PET/CT was performed. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity corresponding to the known right upper lobe primary tumor and the right-sided mediastinal lymph nodes. No other sites of FDG-avid disease were identified to suggest distant metastatic involvement.

A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also completed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

The patient subsequently underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass. Pathology from this procedure confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma.

- An actionable driver mutation was detected in **KRAS (G12C)**.
