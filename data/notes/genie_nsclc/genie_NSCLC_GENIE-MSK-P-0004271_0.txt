**HPI:**
This is a 35-year-old who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of good health until approximately two months prior to presentation, when they developed a persistent, non-productive cough. This was initially presumed to be an upper respiratory infection but did not resolve with a course of antibiotics. Due to the persistence of symptoms, a chest radiograph was obtained, which revealed a right-sided pulmonary opacity. The patient denies any significant hemoptysis, fevers, chills, or unintentional weight loss. They remain fully active and ambulatory, with an ECOG performance status of 0. The patient is a lifetime never-smoker.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed on [De-identified Date]. This study characterized a 4.5 cm spiculated mass in the apical segment of the right upper lobe. No significant mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy was identified.

For definitive tissue diagnosis and staging, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass on [De-identified Date]. Pathologic examination of the tissue confirmed the diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma, consistent with a primary lung origin.

Subsequent staging evaluation included a PET/CT scan from skull base to mid-thigh. This study demonstrated intense FDG avidity within the known right upper lobe primary tumor. Importantly, there was no evidence of FDG-avid locoregional lymphadenopathy or distant metastatic disease. A dedicated MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

Based on this comprehensive workup, the patient is diagnosed with clinical Stage IIA (cT2b N0 M0) non-small cell lung cancer.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy was sent for comprehensive next-generation sequencing and ancillary studies.
-   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable driver alteration was identified. Specifically, testing was negative for mutations or fusions involving EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
-   **PD-L1 Expression:** Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 demonstrated a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1–49% range.
-   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** The TMB was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mutations/megabase).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 35-year-old never-smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IIA (cT2bN0M0) invasive adenocarcinoma of the lung. The patient has an excellent performance status. Staging workup is negative for regional or distant metastatic disease. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for a PD-L1 TPS of 1-49% and the absence of any identified targetable driver alterations. The patient is seen today to review these findings and discuss the prognosis and management options.
