**HPI:**
The patient is a 72-year-old individual presenting for an initial oncology consultation regarding a recent diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The patient initially sought medical attention several weeks ago due to a persistent cough, increasing shortness of breath, and some associated fatigue. Over the past few months, the patient reports an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds. Review of systems was otherwise unremarkable, with no specific complaints of headache, vision changes, bone pain, or abdominal discomfort. Performance status is estimated at ECOG 1, reflecting some symptomatology but largely maintaining daily activities. This consultation represents the first oncology evaluation, and the patient is treatment-naive.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Initial chest imaging, prompted by the presenting symptoms, revealed a suspicious mass in the lung. This was subsequently investigated with a CT-guided biopsy of the lung lesion, which confirmed the diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer with adenocarcinoma histology. Comprehensive staging evaluations were then performed, including a PET/CT scan and a dedicated CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis. These scans revealed the primary lung mass, regional mediastinal lymphadenopathy, and a new hepatic lesion, consistent with distant metastatic disease. An MRI of the brain was also performed as part of the staging workup, which demonstrated no evidence of intracranial metastases. Based on these findings, the patient's cancer is staged as AJCC Stage IV.

**Molecular Studies:**
Tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy underwent molecular profiling. This revealed the presence of an EGFR exon 19 deletion, which is a known sensitizing mutation. Further testing for other common driver mutations was performed and was negative for ALK rearrangement, ROS1 rearrangement, BRAF V600E mutation, MET exon 14 skipping alterations, RET fusions, and NTRK fusions. PD-L1 expression testing was not reported.

**Problem Summary:**
The patient is a 72-year-old individual with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, AJCC Stage IV, with distant metastatic disease to the liver and no evidence of brain metastases. Molecular testing identified an EGFR exon 19 deletion, with other common driver mutations being negative. PD-L1 status is currently unavailable.
