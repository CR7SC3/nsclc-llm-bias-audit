**HPI:**
The patient initially presented approximately three months prior to consultation with a persistent cough, increasing dyspnea on exertion, and a generalized sense of fatigue. Over the subsequent weeks, they also noted an unintentional weight loss of approximately 10 pounds. Evaluation by their primary care provider included a chest X-ray, which revealed a suspicious mass in the right lung. This finding prompted further imaging with a CT scan of the chest, which confirmed a dominant mass in the right upper lobe and suggested regional lymphadenopathy. Following the diagnosis, the patient began experiencing new-onset headaches, described as persistent and diffuse, accompanied by occasional dizziness. A brain MRI was performed as part of the staging workup, which revealed multiple enhancing lesions consistent with brain metastases. The patient currently reports an ECOG performance status of 1, able to perform all usual activities but with some restriction of more strenuous activities. They deny any focal neurological deficits, seizures, or significant cognitive changes at this time.

*   **Pathology:** Biopsy of the right lung mass confirmed non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma subtype.
