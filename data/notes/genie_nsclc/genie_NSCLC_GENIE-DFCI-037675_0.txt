**HPI:**
This is a 78-year-old individual with a history of smoking, who is seen for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient presented to their primary care physician several weeks ago with a persistent, non-productive cough and progressive fatigue. More recently, the patient began experiencing intermittent headaches and a sense of imbalance, which prompted an urgent care evaluation. The patient remains functionally active and is able to perform all activities of daily living with minimal assistance. Appetite has been fair with a mild, unintentional weight loss of approximately 5-7 pounds over the last two months. The patient is a former smoker, having quit over 15 years ago.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
An initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity, leading to further imaging.

A CT of the chest, abdomen, and pelvis with contrast was performed on [De-identified]. This confirmed a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe of the lung. There was associated mediastinal lymphadenopathy noted. No metastatic lesions were identified in the liver, adrenal glands, or bones on the portions of the abdomen and pelvis imaged.

Given the new onset of headaches, a brain MRI with and without contrast was obtained. This revealed three discrete enhancing lesions in the cerebral hemispheres, the largest measuring 1.2 cm in the left frontal lobe, consistent with metastatic disease.

A PET/CT scan was subsequently performed for staging. This demonstrated high FDG avidity in the primary right upper lobe mass and in the mediastinal lymph nodes. No other sites of FDG-avid distant metastatic disease were identified.

A CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe lung mass was performed on [De-identified]. Pathology confirmed non-small cell lung cancer, with features most consistent with adenocarcinoma histology.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue. No actionable driver alterations were identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.

Immunohistochemistry for PD-L1 was positive, with a Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) in the 1–49% range.

Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was determined to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
1.  Stage IV Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer, Adenocarcinoma Histology, with metastases to the brain.
2.  PD-L1 positive (TPS 1-49%).
3.  No identified actionable driver alterations.
4.  Good performance status.
