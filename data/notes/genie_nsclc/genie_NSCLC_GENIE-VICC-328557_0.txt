**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
The initial workup included a chest CT scan which revealed a large primary mass in the right upper lobe, along with significant ipsilateral mediastinal and subcarinal lymphadenopathy. To complete staging, a PET/CT scan was performed. This study confirmed high FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe primary tumor and in multiple mediastinal lymph node stations. Crucially, the PET/CT showed no evidence of FDG-avid disease outside of the thoracic cavity, with no suspicious findings in the liver, adrenal glands, bone, or other distant sites. A staging MRI of the brain with and without contrast was also performed and was negative for intracranial metastases.

For a definitive tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent bronchoscopy with endobronchial ultrasound (EBUS), and a biopsy of a subcarinal lymph node was successfully obtained. Pathology from this specimen confirmed the diagnosis.
