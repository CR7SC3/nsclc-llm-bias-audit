Due to worsening dyspnea, a chest X-ray was obtained which revealed a left-sided lung mass and a moderate pleural effusion.

The patient is here today to discuss these findings. They report ongoing mild dyspnea on exertion but are otherwise feeling well. Their appetite is good and there has been no significant weight loss.
