**DIAGNOSTIC WORKUP:**
A CT of the chest confirmed the presence of a 3.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. There were also several prominent ipsilateral hilar lymph nodes noted.

For the purposes of staging, a PET/CT scan was performed. This demonstrated intense FDG-avidity within the primary right upper lobe mass as well as in several right hilar (N1) lymph nodes. Importantly, there was no evidence of mediastinal (N2/N3) or distant metastatic disease (M0). A subsequent brain MRI with contrast was also performed as part of the standard staging workup and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

To establish a tissue diagnosis, the patient underwent a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Pathology from this procedure confirmed a diagnosis of invasive adenocarcinoma.

The cumulative findings from imaging and pathology are consistent with clinical Stage IIB non-small cell lung cancer.

**MOLECULAR STUDIES:**
Comprehensive next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy specimen.

The analysis did not identify an actionable driver alteration; the tumor was found to be negative for mutations/rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
