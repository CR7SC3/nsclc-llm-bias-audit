**HPI:**
This is a 49-year-old individual with a history of smoking (quit >1 year ago) who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in a usual state of health until approximately three months prior to presentation, when a persistent, dry cough developed. This was initially managed as a respiratory infection without resolution. Over the subsequent weeks, the patient developed progressive, nagging mid-back pain, which was initially attributed to a musculoskeletal strain but worsened in intensity and became constant, interfering with sleep.

Due to the persistence of these symptoms, the patient presented to an outside facility for evaluation. A chest CT scan was performed which revealed a primary mass in the right upper lobe of the lung, along with mediastinal lymphadenopathy and a suspicious lytic lesion in a thoracic vertebral body. A subsequent staging PET/CT was ordered to evaluate the extent of disease.

The patient remains fully ambulatory and is managing self-care but is symptomatic from the back pain and cough, consistent with an ECOG performance status of 1. The patient is here today to discuss the diagnosis and establish care.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
- **CT of the Chest/Abdomen/Pelvis with contrast, [De-identified Date]:** Revealed a 4.2 cm spiculated mass in the right upper lobe. Also noted were multiple enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes and a lytic lesion within the T7 vertebral body, concerning for metastatic disease. No other suspicious lesions were identified in the abdomen or pelvis.

- **PET/CT, [De-identified Date]:** Confirmed intense FDG-avidity in the right upper lobe primary mass (SUV max 12.8), multiple mediastinal nodal stations, and the T7 vertebral body lesion (SUV max 8.9), consistent with metastatic disease. No other sites of FDG-avid disease were seen.

- **MRI of the Brain, [De-identified Date]:** Performed for staging purposes, this showed no evidence of intracranial metastases.

- **CT-Guided Core Biopsy of Right Upper Lobe Mass, [De-identified Date]:** Pathology confirmed invasive, poorly differentiated adenocarcinoma. Immunohistochemical staining was positive for TTF-1, supporting a lung primary.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing (NGS) was performed on the tumor tissue with the following results:
- **Actionable Driver Mutations:** A sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion was detected.
- **Other Genes Tested (Negative):** No mutations or rearrangements were detected in ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, or ERBB2.
- **PD-L1 Expression:** Negative (Tumor Proportion Score <1%).
- **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 49-year-old former smoker with a new diagnosis of Stage IV (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology, with metastasis to bone. Molecular profiling is notable for a sensitizing EGFR exon 19 deletion. PD-L1 expression is negative. The patient is treatment-naive and presents for initial oncologic evaluation.
