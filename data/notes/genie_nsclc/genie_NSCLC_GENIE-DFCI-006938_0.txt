**HPI:**
This is a 61-year-old individual who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation regarding a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient has a significant history of current tobacco use. The diagnosis was prompted by an evaluation for a persistent dry cough over several months. An initial chest radiograph demonstrated a right upper lobe lung nodule, which led to further diagnostic imaging. The patient reports a good baseline functional status and denies constitutional symptoms, including fevers, chills, night sweats, or unintentional weight loss.

**Diagnostic Workup:**
Following the abnormal chest X-ray, a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest was performed, which confirmed the presence of a 2.5 cm spiculated solid nodule in the right upper lobe. There was no associated mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.

For staging, a PET/CT was obtained. This study demonstrated intense FDG-avidity localized to the right upper lobe nodule. There was no abnormal uptake in the mediastinum, contralateral lung, or any distant sites to suggest metastatic disease. A staging MRI of the brain was also performed and was negative for any evidence of intracranial metastases.

On [De-identified Date], the patient underwent a CT-guided percutaneous core needle biopsy of the right upper lobe mass. Histopathologic examination of the tissue confirmed a diagnosis of adenocarcinoma.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor tissue obtained from the biopsy.
- No actionable driver alteration was identified. Testing was negative for mutations or rearrangements in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
- Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB) was found to be intermediate (2–16 mut/Mb).
- PD-L1 expression was not tested on this specimen.

**Problem Summary:**
In summary, this is a 61-year-old current smoker who is treatment-naive and presents for initial oncologic evaluation of a new diagnosis of Stage IA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. The workup has confirmed localized disease in the right upper lobe without evidence of nodal or distant metastatic spread. Molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for an intermediate TMB and no identified actionable genomic alterations.
