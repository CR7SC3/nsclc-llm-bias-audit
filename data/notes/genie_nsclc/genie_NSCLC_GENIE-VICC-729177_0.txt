**HPI:**
This is a 55-year-old individual with an excellent performance status who presents for an initial medical oncology consultation for a new diagnosis of non-small cell lung cancer. The patient was in their usual state of health until approximately two months ago, when they noted the insidious onset of a dry, persistent cough and mild dyspnea on exertion. They presented to their primary care provider, and an initial chest X-ray revealed a right upper lobe opacity.

This prompted a contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, which confirmed a spiculated mass in the right upper lobe with associated prominent mediastinal lymphadenopathy. The patient was subsequently referred for a CT-guided core needle biopsy of the lung mass. Pathology from this procedure established a diagnosis of adenocarcinoma. The patient has been staged with a PET/CT scan and a brain MRI, which confirmed locoregionally advanced disease without evidence of distant metastases.

Currently, the patient reports the cough is stable. They deny hemoptysis, chest pain, fevers, chills, or unintentional weight loss. They remain fully active and independent in all activities of daily living, with an ECOG performance status of 0. Of note, there is no history of tobacco use.

**Diagnostic Workup:**

*   **CT Chest:** A 4.2 cm spiculated mass was identified in the posterior aspect of the right upper lobe. Associated bulky right paratracheal and right hilar lymphadenopathy was noted.
*   **PET/CT Scan ([Date]):** Intense FDG-avidity was demonstrated within the primary right upper lobe mass (SUVmax 12.8) and in multiple ipsilateral mediastinal and hilar lymph node stations, consistent with nodal metastases. No other sites of FDG-avid disease were identified to suggest distant metastatic involvement.
*   **MRI Brain ([Date]):** Contrast-enhanced MRI of the brain was negative for intracranial metastases.
*   **CT-Guided Biopsy, Right Lung Mass ([Pathology Report [De-identified], [Date]):** Core needle biopsy revealed invasive adenocarcinoma, moderately differentiated.

**Molecular Studies:**
Next-generation sequencing was performed on the tumor biopsy specimen.
*   **Driver Mutations:** No actionable oncogenic driver mutations were identified. Testing was negative for alterations in EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET exon 14, RET, NTRK, KRAS, and ERBB2.
*   **Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB):** Intermediate (11 mutations/megabase).
*   **Immune Markers:** A pathogenic loss-of-function mutation was identified in STK11. PD-L1 expression was not tested.

**Problem Summary:**
This is a 55-year-old never-smoker with newly diagnosed, treatment-naive, Stage IIIA (AJCC 8th Edition) non-small cell lung cancer, adenocarcinoma histology. Staging workup confirms locoregionally advanced disease confined to the right hemithorax without distant metastases. Comprehensive molecular profiling of the tumor is notable for the absence of common actionable driver mutations but did reveal an STK11 loss-of-function mutation and an intermediate tumor mutational burden. The patient has an excellent performance status and is seen today to discuss the diagnosis in detail.
